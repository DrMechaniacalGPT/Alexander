worldcraft

describe your perfect world

then try to destroy it

the point is not
to win the argument

the point is to find
what breaks

round one
the dream

describe ordinary life

who has power
how people work
trade
disagree
leave
and change the rules

what does
a good ordinary tuesday
feel like

round two
reality

add

scarcity
unequal ability
different values
crime
corruption
bad luck
disease
technical failure
migration
jealousy
ambition
ignorance

the world must work
without deleting humanity

round three
the villain

give the system
to someone selfish
clever
popular
and patient

what can they capture
hide
centralize
or make irreversible

if the answer is
the right people must stay in charge
you lost

round four
the saint

now give it
to someone good
competent
urgent
and absolutely certain

this may be worse

what can good intentions
justify

round five
the grandchildren

advance twenty five years

the founders are gone
the crisis is forgotten
the incentives remain

what became ritual
what became a racket
what still works

round six
the weird person

test the person
who does not fit

the dissenter
the hermit
the fool
the genius
the outsider
the fanatic
the person
who wants nothing to do with you

a humane system
must survive them too

round seven
scale

run it for

ten people
a thousand
a million
the planet

what must stay local
what needs coordination
what should never be centralized

round eight
exit

someone wants out

can they take
their money
data
identity
work
tools
and reputation

if leaving is impossible
participation was less voluntary
than advertised

round nine
inversion

maximize each virtue
until it becomes a vice

transparency becomes surveillance
safety becomes control
freedom becomes abandonment
equality becomes conformity
efficiency becomes fragility
community becomes exclusion
decentralization becomes chaos
certainty becomes dogma

find the point
where more stops being better

winning

a world wins
when the builder
is willing to change it

the prize is not
a perfect system

it is a system
with known weaknesses
easy exit
limited blast radius
and a method
for becoming less wrong

then play again
