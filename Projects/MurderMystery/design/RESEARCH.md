# Research supporting the design pass

Opened September 26, 2026. This is a focused synthesis, not an exhaustive literature review. Two bounded read-only agents handled primary design reading/critique and then additional reviews/free-material discovery; the integrating researcher reconciled their findings. Sources are not counted as independent validations merely because several reviewers read them. No source proves that our proposed game will be enjoyable.

| ID | Primary source | Evidence and transfer limit |
|---|---|---|
| R01 | [Agency Reconsidered, Wardrip-Fruin et al., 2009](https://dl.digra.org/index.php/dl/article/download/369/369/366) | Conceptual account: agency relates desired actions to available actions and consequences. Read PDF text; not an empirical party study. |
| R02 | [The Motivational Pull of Video Games, Ryan et al., 2006](https://selfdeterminationtheory.org/SDT/documents/2006_RyanRigbyPrzybylski_MandE.pdf) | Four video-game studies; perceived autonomy/competence associated with enjoyment, and multiplayer survey includes relatedness. Context and measures differ from dinner roleplay. Supports investigating, not asserting, our hypothesis. |
| R03 | [Foreseeing Meaningful Choices, Cardona-Rivera et al., 2014](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AIIDE/article/download/12716/12564/16233) | Research agent read primary paper: 88 participants, one online story, six binary choices. Distinguishable situations supported perceived agency with uneven findings across measures. No live multiplayer or moral-ambiguity superiority result. |
| R04 | [YARN, Nór Hernø, 2019](https://www.nordiclarp.org/2019/03/29/your-alternate-relation-narrative-yarn/) | Practitioner method for combining emotional attachment and relationship conflict. Useful for competing commitments; not calibrated psychology. |
| R05 | [Inner Tension, Holkar and Hjort Traxl, 2020](https://www.nordiclarp.org/2020/12/03/inner-tension/) | Practitioner advice on outward identity and inner aspirations. Suggests playable nuance rather than compulsory mannerisms. |
| R06 | [Writing an extra character, Freeform Games](https://www.freeformgames.com/murder-mystery-party-hints-and-tips/how-to-write-an-extra-character-for-your-murder-mystery-game/) | Publisher guidance: connect new roles back into existing materials and provide routes to secrets. Goals recap rather than introduce background. Not independent evidence of results. |
| R07 | [Tips for beginners, Freeform Games, 2014](https://www.freeformgames.com/blog/tips-for-beginners/) | Two specific optional first approaches grounded in background; designer reports experienced guests used them too. Two disclosures is our separate hypothesis. |
| R08 | [Good Cakes, Bad Cakes, Niina Niskanen, 2024](https://nordiclarp.org/2024/07/24/good-cakes-bad-cakes-character-and-contact-design-as-a-factor-of-personal-game-experience/) | Practitioner account of unused contacts and uneven participation in larger larps. Motivates limited reachable relationships; transfer to eight-person dinners untested. |
| R09 | [Storylets, Emily Short, 2019](https://emshort.blog/2019/11/29/storylets-you-want-them/) | Narrative units with conditions and effects; useful authoring vocabulary. Conditions need not mandate moral responses. |
| R10 | [Hollywood Lies development, Freeform Games, 2015](https://www.freeformgames.com/blog/developing-hollywood-lies-part-2/) | Publisher tracks character depth, connections and clue trail separately. Shows existing design tools; not evidence that simulation outperforms them. |
| R11 | [Clocktower rules explanation](https://wiki.bloodontheclocktower.com/Rules_Explanation) | Official hidden-team rules, public/private discussion and continued participation after death. An adjacent format with different victory structure. |

| R14 | [Telling lies, Freeform Games](https://www.freeformgames.com/blog/telling-lies-in-our-murder-mystery-games/) | Lying coexists with explicit compelled-information mechanics. One design approach, not a requirement to compel confessions in ours. |

## Integration, rather than a claim of consensus

Agency research makes consequences worth examining; practitioner methods make relationships and motivations authorable; first-person reviews expose burdens and conflicting preferences. None supplies a validated trait taxonomy, optimal character-card length, disclosure quota or solver percentage. Our strongest inference is to provide understandable opportunities and observe what happens from each player's view.

A further six first-person records, AR1–AR6, are fully coded with provenance in [EXPERIENCE.md](EXPERIENCE.md). Free-game access/terms are recorded separately in [BENCHMARKS.md](BENCHMARKS.md). Those accounts and assets must not be treated as if all are public-domain, independent or current editions.

## What to research next rather than claim now

The six-account supplement confirms conflicting preferences and some practical friction, not our whole theory of play. A later task should code actual conversation behavior from an accessible full playthrough or consented session: approaches, invented claims, corrections, silence, coalition formation and what happens after disclosure. Existing official strategy guides show what players may do, not what ordinary dinner guests typically do. This distinction is why the first policy axes remain hypotheses.
