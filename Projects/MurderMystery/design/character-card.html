Alice

Dependable. Precise. More conflicted than you appear.

You organize things well, and you want people to trust you. Tonight, protecting a friend may come at someone else’s expense.

WHAT MATTERS TO YOU

You owe Sofia a kindness. You also believe nobody should suffer for a false accusation. You decide what to do when these commitments collide.

How you might play Alice

When a story becomes vague, ask for one concrete detail. Your precision sometimes hides a wish to be liked.

Optional cue. Your normal voice is enough. This behavior is not a clue to guilt.

Background you may share

You and Sofia have worked together. She once helped you finish an important project when nobody else would. This is your experience of her; it does not prove her innocence.

Other people do not automatically know this. You can bring it into conversation when it feels relevant.

Two ways to begin

APPROACH SOFIA

Find out what she thinks your protection is costing you—and what she is willing to explain.

APPROACH DANIEL

Ask what people are saying about him and what he needs. Listen before deciding what to promise.

What you know

Sofia asked you to say you were together during a particular morning. You agreed. You were actually apart.

Daniel is now being questioned partly because people trust your account. You must weigh your promise against the harm your lie may cause.

This sketch leaves times and evidence unspecified. The historical facts above stay fixed, even if you choose to lie about them.

Disclosure A · Your choice

When someone challenges your account

If they offer a specific reason to doubt your story, address the discrepancy rather than merely repeating yourself.

You might acknowledge the lie, ask to speak privately, or ask what they intend to do with the information. Another credible response is possible. You do not have to explain Sofia’s reason.

You may disclose earlier. A confrontation does not automatically force a confession.

Disclosure B · Your choice

When someone asks what you owe Sofia

You may explain your promise and what it means to you—or first test whether this person deserves your trust. Decide how much to reveal.

REVEALING THE SECRET DOES NOT END YOUR ROLE

You can try to repair harm to Daniel, ask Sofia to account for her request, seek corroboration, or negotiate how private information is shared. Other people can disagree. Nobody owes you forgiveness.
