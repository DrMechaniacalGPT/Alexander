Incentivized reviews cannot be ruled out; Night of Mystery's preview explicitly offers a testimonial/photo coupon (D013), so its marketing testimonials were not treated as independent corroboration.

## What survives the contradictions

1. **Segment before choosing mechanics.** Some groups want fair deduction and agency; others want prompts, costumes and low pressure. A single ideal format is not established.
2. **The host buys risk reduction.** Clear assembly, casting, timing, contingency instructions and a successful opening may matter as much as prose. This is an inference from these accounts, requiring host observation.
3. **Quality is a system property.** A polished premise can still fail through missing evidence, impossible objectives or confusing items. We have not audited the underlying commercial game files, so complaints remain reports, not independently proven defects.
4. **A premium claim requires comparison.** Free and established products already generate happy evenings. Neither more generated words nor more researcher hours demonstrate improvement.

Next evidence: observe hosts setting up and groups playing; measure preparation, interventions, passive participation and enjoyment separately. Do not turn these 15 accounts into percentages about the market.
