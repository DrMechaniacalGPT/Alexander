## Community accounts and independence

- **D007:** a boardgames discussion contains both frustration with scripts and frustration with chaotic freeform play. One distinction was colleagues versus RPG friends. A commenter disliked random-culprit deduction. These are preference conflicts, not a controlled format comparison.
- **D008/D010:** one experienced-host author prefers small groups and limited scripting, but recommends structured beginner products too. Count the two posts and crossposts as **one voice**, not several confirmations. Another commenter describes enjoying Night of Mystery objectives and public evidence; language/edition disagreement about another game prevents a confident rules conclusion.
- **D009:** a MysteryWild enthusiast reports strong engagement; replies question whether the discussion is advertising. Retain as a lead, not independent proof of satisfaction or fair solvability.
- **D034/D035:** creator discussions describe AI as an authoring aid and a self-published space-race game. They show supply/substitutes, not demand. Their enthusiasm cannot establish buyer willingness to pay.

## What was actually inspected

**One participant-party video transcript** (D015), **one seller-hosted tutorial transcript** (D014), eight search-result metadata records (D016), and a seller video blog (D017). Zero complete video recordings visually inspected; zero video-comment samples. Transcript accounts omit nonverbal behavior, editing and most individual reactions.

Sarah's account (D015) used a free Movie Murder Mystery, customized by merging omitted roles. It describes anxious participants, invented rumors, only 11 of 17 clues found in a timed hunt, host corrections and forgotten card distribution, followed by reported enjoyment. This is one edited retrospective, not a purchase or causal experiment. It usefully separates **robustness under messy communication** from **enjoyment**. A future simulation can challenge the former; it cannot certify the latter.

The Lisa Aspeling publisher transcript (D014) explains casting, staged dinner structure and absence constraints. It establishes the advertised hosting procedure only. D017's embedded play footage was not accessed.
