# Mechanics and failure modes

Primary rules/previews and seller claims were read; commercial packs were not purchased or played. [D-source ledger](../sources.csv).

| Pattern | Inspectable basis | Benefit / likely audience | Failure to test |
|---|---|---|---|
| Scripted dinner rounds | D014 tutorial, D020 box contents; D001/D005 experiences | Explicit turns and low improvisation burden | Long readings, stalls, characters reduced to recital |
| Continuous freeform goals | D011/D012 rules, D040 explanation | Agency, scheming, multiple reasons to talk | Hoarded clues, impossible objectives after secrets spread, adjudication load |
| Staged clues/objectives | D013 preview, D023 product structure | Release key information without fully scripting conversations | Bottleneck host, arbitrary withholding, late evidence dominating deductions |
| App-guided/private prompts | D038 advertised mechanics; D006 participant accounts | Private updates and less guest paper handling | Device access, interface interruptions, support and maintenance; not directly observed |
| Host also plays / stays unspoiled | D031 offer, D036 FAQ, D023 separated documents | Host gets the evening they organize | Accidental solution exposure, inability to adjudicate while blind |
| Social deduction / cooperative case file | D021/D022 adjacent substitutes | Repeat strategic play / compact evidence puzzle | Different job: neither supplies the same dinner-party suspect experience |

These are overlapping dimensions, not exclusive genres. Fixed versus variable culprit is another dimension: D001 contains positive randomized-play testimony; D007 contains rejection. We should choose a fixed solution **for a deduction-oriented promise**, not call it universally superior.

D012 distinguishes character beliefs from objective truth and permits selective disclosure. Any future evidence graph must therefore separate factual evidence, belief, lies and access. D013 makes pacing depend on readiness, suggesting release windows plus recovery instructions rather than a fragile exact-minute script. D036 already advertises guided mingling, limited scripts, fixed resolution, a participating unspoiled host and continued play after a character dies. **That combination is not our invention or a defensible differentiator.**

Paper-first is recommended for the first experiment because it limits production dependencies. It is not an evidence-backed claim that paper always improves immersion. The adjacent 67-person POIROT study (D018) cannot settle paper versus phone at a dinner party.

## Proposed acceptance questions, not proven standards

- Can every participant begin with an understandable action, including reluctant performers?
- Is every necessary inference available through more than one information route, including a public fallback?
- Can players reach a justified accusation without discovering every optional secret?
- Does each role influence another person's choices? Do optional roles remain worthwhile?
- What happens if a core guest cancels, someone withholds information, or the host misses a cue?
- Can an unspoiled host diagnose a stall using instructions that do not reveal the solution?
- Does the group enjoy itself even when few people solve the murder? Do those outcomes diverge by audience?

Later simulations should perturb attendance, disclosure, recall and encounter patterns, and expose assumptions. Passing synthetic agents proves only consistency with their policies. Human tests must assess clarity, enjoyment, embarrassment, trust and value separately. No simulation or game has been built in this phase.
