# M01 pilot catalog — September 26, 2026

## Scope and count

The [catalog](sellers.csv) now records **32 distinct sellers or services** from directly opened offer, catalog, or service pages: independent downloads and hybrids, app/video products, Etsy creators, two new retail/live categories, and a catered dinner. Thirty-one advertise a current offer or catalog; StoryPop's representative page displays a price but also says “Coming Soon.” Several rows retain unknown title-specific prices. The rows are seller claims, not purchased copies, independent play reports, or measured sales.

This is a **purposive pilot plus a second broad search audit**, not completion of M01. The first passes chose illustrative listings rather than consistently applying the preregistered bestseller/alphabetical rule. The follow-up inspected the first 20 results from each of two distinct broad queries and found at least nine additional relevant vendors beyond the original 21, seven with accessible direct pages. This is **positive evidence that discovery has not saturated**. We have now included some shipped and facilitated substitutes but have not sampled board-game alternatives or repeated batches with no new propositions. The catalog reveals format variety and gives us candidates for deeper sampling; its price distribution cannot represent the entire market.

## What the offers actually show

- **Host burden is a selling point.** [MysteryWild](https://mysterywild.com/products/bayside-betrayal-murder-mystery-game) advertises spoiler-free setup; [MurderMysteryParty.com](https://murdermysteryparty.com/) gives the host a victim role while a TV and phones pace the game. These are vendor claims about their products, not yet evidence that customers were satisfied.
- **Format is not a simple paper/app split.** [Masters of Mystery](https://mastersofmystery.com/collections/10-20-player-games/products/mystery-on-the-orient-express-an-agatha-christie-themed-murder-mystery?variant=46184923922682) offers a digital kit with an optional app. [Dagger Games](https://dagger.games/stories) uses phone objectives and act progression. [Freeform Games](https://www.freeformgames.com/product/a-fatal-theory/?currency=USD) sells an unscripted downloadable game with explicit goals and abilities.
- **Culprit assignment varies.** [Broadway Murder Mysteries](https://www.broadwaymurdermysteries.com/products/masquerade-murder) advertises a random culprit, while [My Mystery Kit](https://mymysterykit.com/products/mystery-at-the-groove-gala) advertises a fixed one. We cannot call either mechanic superior from catalog pages.
- **A three-hour adult party overlaps many offers but does not define them.** The sample includes 90-minute app sessions, roughly two-hour printables, and three-to-four-hour freeform play. Three smaller-cast adjacent products are deliberately labeled in the CSV.
- **Visible prices span different currencies, promotions, and units.** Some are USD game downloads, some GBP or EUR, two AUD, one per 12-hour session, and some selected player tiers. Do not average them or apply a shop's lifetime sales count to one price.
- **Physical and live offers sell different labor.** [My Mystery Party's boxed version of the same title](https://mymysteryparty.com/products/homicide-at-the-tacky-sweater-shindig-boxed-kit) displays $119.95 for an 8–12 box compared with $43.95 for its PDF, though the standard box still requires some envelope preparation. [Baker Street Mystery](https://bakerstreetmystery.com/faq/) lists $375–$600 for a facilitator with 6–19 guests; [No Thyme to Cook](https://www.nothymetocook.com/murder-mystery) lists $1,250 up to ten guests for a venue experience. These are adjacent services, not directly comparable unit prices.
- **Even the candidate worlds have close neighbors.** [Bespoke Mysteries](https://bespokemysteries.com/) advertises “Silicon Succession” with print, private phone packets, and timed live tiers. [DigitalBearded's made-to-order auction mystery](https://www.etsy.com/listing/4520316768/personalized-hotel-murder-mystery-game) advertises eight required and four optional roles, print/phone/hybrid delivery, and a fixed clue solution. This does not invalidate either of our brainstormed themes; it raises the originality and differentiation bar.

## Immediate corrections to the earlier pitch

“Phone dossiers” and “host can play” are established offer claims in the observed set; neither is a defensible differentiator by itself. A premium print-at-home price is possible among these listings, but this sample cannot tell us what price an unknown new title could command. The previous 14,456-shop-sales arithmetic remains a scenario about a hypothetical shop-wide average, not evidence of annual revenue for one game. Etsy says the public shop count includes open and completed orders and excludes canceled orders: https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360024112734-Where-Can-I-Find-My-Total-Number-of-Sales .
