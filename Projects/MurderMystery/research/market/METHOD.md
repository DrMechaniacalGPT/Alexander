# M01 method — seller and product map

Prepared September 26, 2026, before compiling the catalog. Target: products a US host might buy for an in-person adult murder mystery party with about 8–12 guests and roughly three hours of play. Include close substitutes, but label them separately.

## Discovery surfaces and query families

Search independent seller sites, Etsy and other marketplaces, app/browser products, boxed kits, and live-hosted alternatives. Use combinations of “murder mystery party”, “dinner party”, “8–12 players”, “printable”, “boxed”, “freeform”, “app”, “host can play”, and “three hours”; search both broad phrases and platform-specific results. Record each query, date, surface, and result window in the search log. Search result order is a discovery route, not a quality ranking.

Map at least 20 distinct sellers across these strata if verifiable: independent download specialists, boxed/fulfilled sellers, marketplace creators, app/video hybrids, and indirect live-hosted or board-game substitutes. Do not fill a stratum with near-duplicate brands. Sample the first two result pages where accessible, plus deliberate searches for newer and less prominent sellers. Note inaccessible pages and exclusions. Stop expanding only after all strata have evidence and two successive search batches add no materially new format or buyer proposition; record the batches.

For each seller, choose one eligible representative title: seller-designated bestseller if visible, otherwise the first eligible title alphabetically. Log this selection rule and deviations. A title outside the target range can be included as an adjacent substitute if labeled. Record direct listing URL, access date, seller, title, format, price/currency and whether discounted, guest range, claimed duration, host role, mechanics, review count, and unknown fields. Never infer missing rules from marketing adjectives. Each source gets an ID in the common ledger.

## Interpretation

Seller sites establish offers and claims, not actual player satisfaction or sales. Etsy shop counts cover the shop, not a particular game or time period. The catalog will describe the supply landscape and price distribution; M02–M05 address experience and economics. Summarize by format and host burden, identify exceptions, and report coverage and uncertainty before choosing a concept.
