Asking prices show competitive offers, not willingness to pay for ours. No sales, profit or annual market size has been inferred from review counts. Free/customized alternatives and established publishers both raise the bar. A new theme or “AI-generated personalized story” alone is a weak thesis.

**Possible wedge to test:** a first-time host can confidently assemble and run a fair, engaging small dinner mystery with low preparation. Competitors already promise convenience; the opportunity depends on demonstrably better execution for a defined audience, not an empty category.
