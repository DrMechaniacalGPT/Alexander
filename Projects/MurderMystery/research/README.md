# Research record

Current coverage: **planning only**. No seller census, review sample, interviews, or playtests have been completed in this repository. The earlier chat research supplies leads to verify, not a source ledger.

## Evidence chain

1. `sources.csv`: one row per located source with ID, query, discovery route, date, URL, author/seller, type, and access limits.
2. Task files: selection method, observations tied to source IDs, conflicting evidence, exclusions, and a concise synthesis.
3. `DECISION.md`: claims and confidence, linked to task files; commercial arithmetic with time basis and assumptions.
4. Project `README.md`: the short current answer, with links downward.

For every research pass, record the exact search terms, search surface, date, result window, inclusion rule, excluded duplicates, and stopping reason. Repeated anecdotes and syndicated descriptions are not independent evidence. Quote sparingly and link to the original. Do not infer a defect rate from complaints without a denominator or a market size from seller review counts.

## Claim notation

- **Observed:** directly checked in a dated primary listing, ruleset, recording, or review.
- **Reported:** a person's account; say who, when, and in what context.
- **Inferred:** our interpretation of observed/reported material; state alternatives.
- **Scenario:** an explicit input to a model, not an estimate presented as fact.
- **Untested:** a design hypothesis waiting for research or a playtest.

The source ledger is intentionally empty at this gate. Research task PRs will populate it and their corresponding market/experience folders. A future reader should be able to reproduce the search and disagree with the synthesis without guessing where a number came from.
