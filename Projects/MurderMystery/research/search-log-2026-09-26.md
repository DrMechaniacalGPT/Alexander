## Limitations carried into the recommendation

English-language search; purposive title selection; vendor-heavy documentation; self-selected, possibly incentivized testimonials; unknown versions; no verified transaction dataset; little negative app evidence; no full video/comment comparison; no observed host study, paid acquisition test or production quote. Those gaps are not filled by persona exercises. Additional searches may find more sellers; only human and commercial experiments can resolve the decisive product-specific unknowns.
