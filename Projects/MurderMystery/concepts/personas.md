# Adversarial perspectives

This is an analytical exercise by one researcher. These are **not independent agents, interviewees, survey responses or simulated customers**. Original C02's independent-perspective/human-interview intent remains unfulfilled. The purpose is to expose tests, not manufacture agreement.

| Perspective | Immortality objection | Lot 13 objection | Last Broadcast objection | Resolving evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| First-time host | Will I need to explain biology? | Will I need to manage money/bids? | Who runs the recording equipment? | Timed unassisted setup and first act; record interventions |
| Shy player | An elite persona feels like an acting audition | Negotiating with strangers is intimidating | Am I expected to perform on microphone? | Optional conversation starters; private postgame interview, not host proxy |
| Deduction enthusiast | Does invented science permit arbitrary answers? | Can physical evidence settle ownership/timing? | Is the key clue hidden in an inaudible line? | Independent readers attempt supported accusations with complete evidence |
| Casual social guest | I want a fun dinner, not homework about mortality | Financial details sound dry | Shared listening might interrupt conversation | Observe engagement and reading burden; compare stated preference to play |
| Returning buyer | Is this another wealthy-people secret society? | Many auction mysteries already exist | Radio novelty may last only one evening | Blind pitch comparison including established/free alternatives |
| Cost-conscious buyer | Why pay if a model can produce the story? | Why not choose a free kit? | Why pay for voice acting before game quality is proven? | Test a concrete value promise and real paid offer later |
| Alexander steward | Does a closed content moat fit our open-source purpose? | Is this teaching reusable methods or merely writing a story? | Are we building media infrastructure prematurely? | Explicit distribution/licensing decision; track reusable artifacts and real cost |

Sources motivating concerns include scripts/freeform preference conflicts (D001/D007), host/role friction (D003/D004), close competing positioning (D036) and existing free/generated substitutes (D003/D039). The theme-specific objections above are hypotheses.

**Strongest challenge:** established games may already satisfy this audience. Our research process could be more elaborate without producing a better evening. Only comparative host/player evidence should earn further investment. If the owner prefers another business idea with easier distribution or clearer unmet need, these findings do not justify forcing this one forward.
