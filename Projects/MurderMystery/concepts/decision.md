# Proposed concept discussion — not approval to build

**Recommend discussing The Immortality Club and Lot 13, with only a small validation commitment next.** Immortality has the stronger immediate tension; Lot 13 is easier to explain. [Rubric and sensitivity](longlist.md) show how close this judgment is. Neither has customer validation.

Proposed audience: adults hosting a small dinner/mingle party, mixed comfort with acting, interested in a fair fixed mystery and useful conversational goals. Start by testing **eight core participants** rather than promising flexible 8–12. Additional roles and cancellation resilience require evidence; optional guests must not become decorative extras. Target roughly three hours with dinner, to be measured, not promised.

Proposed medium for the first playable experiment, **after owner agreement**: a printable pack with short role briefs, physical clues, staged public fallbacks and unspoiled host directions. This controls initial production cost. It does not rule out later app/box/video formats or prove that paper is superior. Do not build an engine before understanding the evening.

The commercial claim to test is reduced host effort plus a fair, socially engaging mystery. A premium theme or AI authoring is insufficient. Mischief already claims much of the format; we must beat a credible baseline on observable outcomes.

## Questions for the owner at the gate

1. Does this audience and style fit the product you imagined? What experience or setting constraints have you held back?
2. Which appeal is stronger to you: Immortality's scarcity and moral tension, or Lot 13's approachable tangible mystery? A different direction remains available.
3. Should the eventual complete game be open-source alongside the method? The repository currently has a root MIT license; do not assume exclusive paid narrative content. Agree distribution terms before publishing a full game. Convenience, tested packaging and support might be saleable, but are not yet proven revenue.
4. What maximum cash/time investment makes a validation experiment worthwhile versus other project ideas?

No game, sales page, interviews, recruitment or purchases are authorized by this memo itself. The existing owner instruction requires this conversation before construction.
