## Finalist comparison

Judgment rubric, not consumer data. Scores 1–5, higher favorable. Weights: immediate hook 25%, plausible conflicting goals 25%, tangible evidence 20%, low explanation/access burden 20%, low production burden 10%. Scores are one researcher's integrated judgments; uncertainty is at least a point, not a calibrated interval.

| Lot 13 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4.20 |
| Last Broadcast | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 3 | 3.70 |
| Last Safe House | 4 | 5 | 3 | 2 | 4 | 3.65 |
| Launch Dinner | 4 | 4 | 4 | 2 | 3 | 3.50 |

The 0.10 lead is not meaningful market evidence. Increasing ease to 35% and reducing hook to 10% gives Lot 13 **4.20**, Immortality **4.00**. If accessibility dominates, the recommendation changes. No pricing premium or demand is assigned to these scores.

Immortality is a provisional conversation starter because scarcity can connect the dinner's relationships and conflicting goals. Lot 13 is the lower-explanation alternative. The owner's preferences and actual host reactions should choose between them; desk research has not selected a commercial winner.
