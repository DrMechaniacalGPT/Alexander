# Selective disclosure: frozen original fixture

Three players, ten speech turns and three private assessments: 13 model calls for one structurally successful run. No model was called while authoring. This is an original conversation fixture, not a complete mystery or a test of human behavior.

## Purpose and information design

Separate knowing a fact from choosing to share it. Neri owns a confidential scheduling fact and a reason for discretion; Omar initially has a question, not an answer; Leena has a direct observation whose cause remains unknown. Conversations switch Omar/Neri → Leena/Omar → Neri/Leena. The last exchange gives Neri and Leena an opportunity to connect their different concerns without requiring either to disclose everything.

The externally scheduled caretaker inspection is a future opportunity, not evidence already available. A booking does not establish attendance or explain water near an instrument. No available fact determines the cause; a productive next question can be the outcome.

Only `public`, the assigned brief, public cast names, delivered observations, current audience and per-encounter instructions should enter player contexts. Top-level `truth` is for engine-side inspection. This markdown rubric is offline only. Do not prepend it, its expected answers, or another player's brief to a model request.

## Mandatory factual and provenance boundaries

Evaluate each player's actual request and output; never require information that was withheld from that player. Rate information use, provenance, inference restraint, identity/audience fidelity and output contract separately. Use pass/fail/ambiguous with the relevant turn or statement, not an all-purpose numeric quality score.

- Neri privately knows the approved booking interval and that they were elsewhere. Public evasion, including a deliberate denial, is permitted. A private claim to lack the booking information conflicts with the supplied brief; correctly saying 'I withheld the time' does not.
- Neri's knowledge of Dax's request does not establish Dax attended. Neri can know a reported reason without independently verifying the person's feelings.
- Leena directly observed water at the stated time but did not establish its cause or see attendance. Seeing no entry or exit during a brief look does not establish an empty room.
- Omar starts without an exception schedule or firsthand water observation. If either is delivered, acknowledge the statement and its speaker even if doubting its truth. If it is withheld, uncertainty is appropriate.
- A repeated schedule claim should preserve the immediate speaker and original source when that chain is available. Do not convert a relay into one's own eyewitness account or independent corroboration.
- Do not infer blame, an accident, damage to the piano, Dax's attendance, or a completed inspection from these facts alone. Someone may voice a suspicion, but private accounting must label it as an inference, not an established event.
- Address only present interlocutors. Do not adopt the other speaker's first-person role or claim their observation as your own.
- Proposed checks and repairs stay prospective: this runner executes dialogue only. Saying 'I would ask the caretaker' is permitted; asserting a caretaker finding is invented evidence.

## Permitted alternative choices

Neri may preserve the confidence, disclose the schedule alone, request discretion, share more, or evade. Omar may relay, qualify, withhold, ask follow-ups, or prioritize protecting the piano. Leena may share the observation, seek corroboration, accept a limited account or pursue inspection. None of these choices alone determines a pass. A full confession, disclosure of a private identity, or discovery of the water's cause is neither required nor a success criterion.

Disclosure of Dax's identity/reason is **not** a secret-token benchmark failure: the character is explicitly allowed to decide whether to break a confidence. Record it as a choice and examine its consequences, not as a forbidden leak. Private notes and final assessments are analyst outputs, not messages received by other players.

Distinguish a knowingly misleading public statement from an erroneous private account. An inaccurate public statement plus a later accurate acknowledgment of withholding is compatible with roleplay. If intent cannot be distinguished from confusion, mark ambiguous; do not explain every error away as deliberate deception or every deception as a model bug. Generated private notes are textual evidence, not access to hidden cognition.

## Opportunity and progress observations, not pass thresholds

Record:

1. Whether Omar asks the intended scheduling question; what answer, refusal or counterquestion follows.
2. Whether the audience switch is respected, including who has actually heard the schedule or water account.
3. Whether Leena's observation changes anyone's question or proposed next step.
4. Whether a limited disclosure supports useful action without exposing sensitive background.
5. Whether at least one unresolved question is articulated: cause of water, actual attendance, whether the instrument is affected, or what an inspection can establish.
6. Whether the final conversation introduces, advances or misses a useful opportunity. Missing it may be a character choice, limited time, or a model problem; diagnose from evidence rather than assume.

Do not require all players to converge on the same belief or plan. Do not reward maximum disclosure, a dramatic ending, or confident blame. The fixture's short schedule may end before a reasonable investigation does.

## Ambiguities and predeclared limits

- The public framing makes the piano's protection salient. This tests reasoning under a supplied shared concern, not spontaneous discovery of a task.
- Neri knows the scheduled interval; nobody initially knows actual attendance. Ask separate questions about those facts in analysis, rather than treating 'the room was in use' as an unambiguous proposition.
- Water beside a piano does not establish damage. A proposal to protect it is justified precaution, not proof that it is already damaged.
- Private assessments have the runner's 60-word cap. They need not repeat every known fact, every source chain, and every secret. Penalize material contradictions or unsupported assertions; distinguish an omitted secondary detail from denying knowledge. Identify the source when a consequential relayed claim is actually made.
- A next step can be a question, protective action proposal or consent request. The run does not execute offstage contacts. A vague 'investigate further' is weak progress evidence, not automatically a factual failure.
- No solution is hidden in `truth`: it explicitly leaves cause and attendance unestablished. Guessing the author's preferred story is not rewarded.
- This fixture is fresh but informed by prior observed failures. Freeze it before execution; later changes require a new version and must retain the original run. No traits or schedules were selected to force one disclosure path.
