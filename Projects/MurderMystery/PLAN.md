# Design and build plan

September 26 revision: the owner wants a creative simulation-and-game experiment, followed by a useful product. Co-design small situations and the machinery that explores them. A complete paper game is no longer a prerequisite for writing the simulator, and static defect detection is not the only way the simulator can earn its keep.

Gates organize decisions; they do not require stopping between every routine subtask. The current user request ends at planning. Later execution should progress through its agreed slice without repeatedly asking for parameters that the agent can reasonably choose.

## First execution slice

[W01–W04](TASKS.md#first-execution-slice-w01w04) specify outputs, dependencies and acceptance checks. Author one original dilemma with reciprocal roles and meaningful consequences. Implement exact information routing using stub responses, then connect the installed local model. Record failures and resource usage. Keep the first live experiments within [explicit caps](design/EXPERIMENTS.md).

An existing free scenario is a useful independent challenge, but adapting a whole game first would combine difficult encoding, rights, moderation and behavioral questions. [W08 and the benchmark comparison](design/BENCHMARKS.md) separate mapping from adaptation. Neither a tiny fixture nor a public scenario supplies human outcome ground truth.

## Explore choices without predetermining them

Track fixed history, character knowledge/commitments, participant policies, information exposure, accessible memory and beliefs separately. Compare different legitimately received information before the same dilemma, then study whether that information is encountered naturally. Confession, bargaining and concealment are possible interpretations, not mandatory branches or quotas.

Pairwise scheduling comes before group mode. Vary initiative, disclosure and attention one at a time before combining more profiles. Factions, improvisation and rumors are extensions, with explicit distinctions between claims and canonical evidence. Report each player's opportunities and what remains after disclosure, not merely whether someone guessed correctly.

Preserve chosen costs and failures. Add independent discovery routes to prevent accidental stalls, without automatically undoing a meaningful refusal. The evaluator sees full truth; player contexts never do. Simulations can expose assumptions and produce creative material without establishing population probabilities or measuring fun.

## Keep research connected to design

The new [source synthesis](design/RESEARCH.md) and [review supplement](design/EXPERIENCE.md) supplement the earlier market record. Contradictory preferences are design inputs: freeform freedom may delight one group and burden another. Actual-play coding is W09, a parallel research opportunity rather than a prerequisite for every experiment. Strategy guides describe possible behavior, not observed frequencies.

Use sources to challenge our favorite hypotheses. A wide search result count is not market size; persona critique is not an interview. Preserve exact versions, access failures, publisher interests and whether a claim comes from observation or advice. Historical incomplete discovery tasks stay visible instead of being silently declared exhaustive.

## From situation to product

Choose a theme with the owner after the first traces are useful. Immortality Club, Lot 13 and alternatives remain candidates. Write one coherent scene and review role workload, accessibility, timing, shared orientation, cancellation behavior and the distinction between individually and collectively discoverable conclusions. Extend into a full case only when the slice warrants it.

Reader checks can start early; real parties later test comprehension, enjoyment, fairness and host effort separately from solving the case. Few groups can still reveal valuable surprises, without calibrating a universal psychological model. Contacting volunteers requires authorization; writing a recruitment plan does not authorize outreach.

A public process account should show actual experiments and failures, independently of Alexander branding. Views, interested developers, qualified hosts and buyers are different outcomes. Decide asset licensing and finished-product convenience deliberately. Revise the economics for simulator maintenance, writing, video production, acquisition, fulfillment and support before a commercial commitment.

## Working rhythm

The markdown [task board](TASKS.md) is the single current status record. Implement small dependent PRs, retain versioned fixtures and raw traces, and keep the README and one-page run reports concise. A polished transcript is not a result unless the conditions and limitations are inspectable. Re-estimate later work from the realistic-context benchmark rather than committing now to a full-product delivery date.
