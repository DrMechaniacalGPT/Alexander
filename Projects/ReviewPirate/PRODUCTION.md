# Production System

content factory
without factory content

## constraint

YouTube currently requires monetized content
to be original and authentic

mass-produced
generic
or minimally varied AI content
can be ineligible for monetization

reused footage also needs
meaningful original commentary
and transformation

official references

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/2801973
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/15447836
https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398

so

automate the labor
not the point of view

## production loop

### 1 topic radar

score candidate episodes on

- purchase intent
- audience size
- product price
- source richness
- disagreement
- launch timing
- affiliate availability
- visual potential

### 2 research packet

finish the source ledger
before scripting

deliverables

- consensus
- disagreements
- hidden variables
- failure modes
- reviewer map
- buyer segments
- source list

### 3 story

the script needs a reason to exist

not

here are ten features

better

five respected reviewers
picked four different winners
because they are quietly answering
four different questions

### 4 script

rough structure

0:00 hook
0:20 what we researched
0:40 surprising disagreement
2:00 first decisive finding
4:00 second decisive finding
6:00 long-term / owner evidence
7:30 who should buy what
8:30 what remains uncertain
9:00 sources / close

length is earned
not targeted

### 5 narration

test

- ElevenLabs
- Descript
- other current high-quality voice tools

quality bar

it cannot sound like
a man trapped in a LinkedIn webinar

the voice should feel
recognizable
dry
and human enough
to carry jokes

### 6 visuals

priority

1. original charts and diagrams
2. product press assets we have rights to use
3. source headlines / test results used for commentary
4. short transformed review clips when legally appropriate
5. generated visual transitions / pirate flavor
6. stock footage

do not generate fake product behavior
that looks like evidence

### 7 edit

candidate tools

- Descript for transcript-led editing
- CapCut for fast social production
- Premiere / Resolve if the workflow outgrows them
- Canva / Figma for thumbnails and charts
- Python or simple scripts for repeatable data graphics

choose based on speed
after producing one real episode

### 8 QA

before publishing

- every important factual claim has a source
- quotes are accurate
- affiliate relationships are disclosed
- clips are meaningfully transformed
- AI disclosure is set when required
- no generated visual is presented as real testing
- title is supported by the video
- thumbnail does not promise a fake result

### 9 publish

long form first

then cut findings into Shorts

Shorts are trailers
not the research product

### 10 postmortem

track

- impressions
- click-through rate
- average view duration
- retention curve
- search terms
- suggested-video sources
- subscribers per thousand views
- affiliate clicks
- conversion
- revenue per thousand views
- comments that reveal unanswered purchase questions

the next episode
should be changed
by the previous one

full script-to-video generation
for episode 001

the research
needs to be visible
