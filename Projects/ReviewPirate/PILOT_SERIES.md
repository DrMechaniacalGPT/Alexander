# Five Episode Test

the channel should not
accidentally become

the robot vacuum channel

the first five episodes
are a business experiment

each category tests
a different version
of Review Pirate

## selection rule

a good test category has

- active purchase intent
- meaningful purchase price
- enough audience to discover us
- multiple serious review ecosystems
- real disagreement
- evidence deeper than a spec sheet
- affiliate paths
- enough future products
  that research can compound

the five episodes
should not all fail
for the same reason

status

working hook

lesson to measure

## 005 — mattresses

working hook

**Can You Trust "Best Mattress" Reviews?**

alternative

**They Tested 2,000 Mattresses. But "Best" Is Still Personal.**

what this tests

the most difficult
Review Pirate problem

subjective preference
plus
commercial incentives

research anchor

Sleep Foundation says
its lab has tested
about 2,000 mattresses

with

- pressure mapping
- heat retention
- motion isolation
- edge support
- multiple body types
- multiple sleep positions
- field testing
- owner feedback

it also openly discloses
affiliate relationships
across the sleep industry

Consumer Reports
uses a different
lab approach
and emphasizes
body type
sleep position
and measured firmness

why Review Pirate helps

the wrong conclusion is

affiliate links
therefore fake

the better question is

which parts
of mattress reviewing
can be measured

which parts
are personal

and how should
commercial incentives
change our confidence

business test

high purchase value

affiliate-heavy category

deep consumer confusion

this deliberately tests
whether the channel
can discuss incentives
without becoming
a conspiracy channel

research question

can we review
the review industry itself
and still give
useful buying guidance
