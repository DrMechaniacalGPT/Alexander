## outlier lesson — robot vacuums

recent breakout results
are dominated by

- new product launches
- product-vs-product comparisons
- "what actually matters before you buy"
- dramatic test framing

one useful example

a small channel's
DJI ROMO P2 video

the World's Most Advanced Robot Vacuum "

the channel baseline
