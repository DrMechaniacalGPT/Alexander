# Economics

the first business question

can one research packet
produce enough useful attention
and purchase intent
to pay for the next one

do not solve this
with imaginary CPMs

measure it

## revenue stack

### stage zero

external affiliate links

this can work
before YouTube monetization

current examples
checked September 2026

Roborock

- official affiliate program
- 4 to 7 percent commission
- says average order value is $400+
- 35 day cookie
- runs through Impact

source

https://us.roborock.com/pages/roborock-affiliate-program

eufy

- official affiliate program
- advertises 7 to 15 percent
- 30 day cookie

source

https://www.eufy.com/affiliate

MOVA US

- official affiliate program
- advertises a product-dependent range
  up to 20 percent
- says average order value exceeds $600
- CJ / Webgains

source

https://us.mova.tech/pages/affiliate-program

Amazon Associates

standard rates vary by category

the current standard table includes

- Home 3 percent
- Headphones 3 percent
- Kitchen 4.5 percent
- Televisions 2 percent
- PC / components 2.5 percent

source

https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/node/topic/GRXPHT8U84RAYDXZ

important

do not assume
which Amazon category
a particular product lands in

verify the item

direct manufacturer programs
may be materially better
than Amazon

also

the highest commission
must never become
the highest recommendation

## stage one

YouTube Partner Program

YouTube currently says
Watch Page ads pay creators
55 percent of net ad revenue

but

there is no guaranteed RPM

so RPM belongs
in the measurement sheet

not the business plan

source

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/72902

## stage two

YouTube Shopping

for eligible creators

YouTube Shopping can

- tag products in videos
- report shopping analytics
- pay commissions
- expose campaign offers
  with elevated rates or bonuses

eligibility requires
being in YPP
plus the relevant subscriber threshold
and other channel requirements

source

https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/13376398

## stage three

sponsors

later

sponsors can buy

- clearly labeled ad reads
- episode support
- access to audience attention

they cannot buy

- product rank
- source selection
- omitted criticism
- a conclusion

the fastest way
to kill this business

would be to make
the research visibly purchasable

## formulas

affiliate revenue

views
x affiliate click rate
x merchant conversion rate
x average order value
x commission rate

episode contribution

affiliate revenue
+ ad revenue
+ shopping revenue
+ sponsor revenue
- direct production cost

unmeasured assumptions
in the numerator
and call it strategy

## production economics

the first episode
will be inefficient

that is fine

record every hour

the question is

which work compounds

research should compound

reviewer profiles
methodology notes
source-quality judgments
brand affiliate relationships
visual templates
editing patterns

should make later episodes
faster
without making them
more generic

the goal is not

make video fast

the goal is

make the second deep video
cheaper than the first

## five episode test

do not judge the business
from one upload

after five serious episodes

review

### discovery

did any episode
earn meaningful search
or suggested traffic
without an existing audience

### retention

did people stay
through the research

or leave
when the methodology started

### commerce

did purchase-intent viewers
click

did clicks convert

which merchants converted

### repeatability

did production hours
fall

did the research library
actually compound

### audience signal

did comments ask

- for another category
- for specific products
- for updates
- for the sources

or did people
just say nice video

### decision

after five

scale

if there is
discovery
retention
and commercial intent

change format

if research is valuable
but video packaging is weak

kill

if the content
does not earn attention
or purchase intent
after honest iteration

there are more ideas

## category selection economics

a strong category has

- purchase intent
- enough price to matter
- enough search audience
- rich source ecosystem
- frequent product turnover
- useful disagreements
- affiliate access
- repeatable episode opportunities

affiliate rate alone
is not the category score

twenty percent
of no purchases
is still spiritual wealth
