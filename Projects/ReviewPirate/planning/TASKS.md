### R1 — Normalize and audit the inherited evidence

Dependencies: execution approval. Output: `research/claims.csv`, `research/sources.csv`, `research/search-log.md`, corrected matrix schema and `research/AUDIT_RESULTS.md`.

- R1.1 Assign source/test/claim IDs; deduplicate URLs, owner updates and syndicated or repeated test results. Keep publisher and independent test group distinct.
- R1.2 Separate observation, source assertion, inference and anecdote. Record URL, access date, publication/update date, exact section or video timecode, model variant, region, firmware if available, methods and incentives. Mark unavailable information unknown.
- R1.3 Audit every script paragraph, title assertion and graphic, including the blank-cell error and X60 variant ambiguity. Enumerate all products recommended outside the eight-product hook.
- R1.4 Define matrix states and exclusions explicitly. Never infer “not tested” from a failed search. Preserve historical snapshots rather than overwriting dates.

Done when every material draft claim is mapped to evidence or an explicit repair/remove task; source counts can be reproduced; unknowns are visible. No source count is a quality gate.

### R2 — Recheck rankings, product identity and actual coverage

Dependencies: R1 schema. Output: `research/rankings.md`, matrix/source updates and `research/coverage-audit.md`.

- R2.1 Directly check all eight opening sources and the exact category, candidate pool, market and observation date. Verify The Hook Up conclusion from accessible original video/transcript with timecodes; search snippets alone cannot verify a quote.
- R2.2 Search each of the core 64 source/product pairs with a logged query and access outcome. Include results that weaken our preferred story; do not stop after finding each source's winner. Log unretrievable/paywalled material without bypassing it.
- R2.3 Verify regional variants using manufacturer documentation. Keep Pro/Max and family names separate unless equivalence is documented.
- R2.4 Test the purported ranking-change story using comparable dated evidence. Replace it if snapshots or causal attribution cannot be established.

Done when the opening's exact count is defensible, all 64 cells have an audit disposition (unknown is allowed), and no variant is silently merged. Not every cell must become filled.

### R3 — Explain disagreements and build useful buyer decisions

Dependencies: R2; may begin on verified subsets. Output: `research/disagreements.md`, `research/buyer-decisions.md`.

- R3.1 Compare competing hypotheses: candidate pools, test protocol, weightings, price/market, evidence age, genuine measurement conflict, and insufficient reporting.
- R3.2 Develop at least three consequential case studies tied to buyer needs: carpet/pets; hard floors/mopping/maintenance; clutter/obstacles. Use overlapping products where possible. Add low-cost/value alternatives so premium rankings do not define the entire market.
- R3.3 For each case preserve agreement, contradiction, likely explanation, evidence against that explanation and what remains unknown. Distinguish documented weights from inferred preferences. Do not average unlike scores or count votes as confidence.
- R3.4 Verify manufacturer/support information for finalists: availability, warranty/parts, recurring consumables, maintenance and relevant privacy/cloud dependence. State any purchase-relevant blind spot.
- R3.5 Write buyer paths with a priority, shortlist or decision rule, tradeoff, price/region/date, confidence and “do not choose this if…” limitation. Prefer fewer well-supported paths to many decorative categories.

Done when the viewer can make at least three clearer decisions and each central explanation has a tested alternative. If “methodology explains everything” fails, change the thesis. An unexplained conflict can itself be the honest finding.

### R4 — Deepen ownership and neglected-dimension evidence

Dependencies: R1; works alongside R2/R3. Output: `research/owners.md` and source records.

- R4.1 Before collection, fix a sampling protocol for the eight anchor products: Reddit, accessible retailer reviews, support communities, and long-term creator reports. Search product + ownership/update/problem/support terms; record queries, sort, date window and screening order.
- R4.2 Initial bounded screen per product: first five relevant community results and, where accessible, first three recent positive and first three recent critical retailer reviews under recorded sort/filter settings. Deduplicate author/thread/cross-posts. This is a diagnostic sample, not representative prevalence research.
- R4.3 Code ownership duration, firmware/region if stated, verified/incentivized badges when available, household conditions, problem, outcome and updates. Follow the same thread through reversals. Missing badges remain unknown.
- R4.4 Compare mature and new products separately. Seek counterexamples to recurring failure stories, then corroborate consequential issues in a second independent source type when possible. Retain single reports as anecdotes if not corroborated.

Done when coverage and access failures are accounted for across all eight products and relevant buyer paths. Never estimate failure rates from this sample or treat no complaints as reliability. If a major conclusion changes, expand the relevant stratum rather than blindly expanding everything.

### R5 — Test discovery intent and packaging hypotheses

Dependencies: R1 scope; can precede final research conclusions. Output: `research/discovery-refresh.md`, `packaging/OPTIONS.md`.

- R5.1 Preserve the old vidIQ estimates as historical; capture new tool inputs/outputs, date and geography if a refresh is authorized within available credits. Keyword metrics are estimates, title scores heuristics, not CTR tests.
- R5.2 Compare broad “best robot vacuum” intent with pet-hair, mopping and reviewer-disagreement queries. Inspect a bounded set of ten relevant recent competing videos; log query/order, date, channel size, promise and what the viewer actually receives. Views are not sales and survivorship is a limitation.
- R5.3 Draft three distinct title/thumbnail pairs: disagreement, buyer consequence, surprising evidence. Keep the searchable product phrase and an earned curiosity gap. Exact “8 winners” packaging requires R2 success.

Done when one package and one alternative have a reasoned audience/accuracy case. Virality remains unproven until audience exposure. Avoid collecting more title scores as a substitute for editorial judgment.

### R6 — Write the story upward from the evidence

Dependencies: R2–R4 synthesis; R5 informs packaging. Output: revised episode `SCRIPT.md`, paragraph/scene claim links, `STORYBOARD.md` and read-through notes.

- R6.1 Lead with a buyer consequence and a concrete conflict. Reveal a useful decision in the first minute; delay the complete matrix until viewers understand why it matters.
- R6.2 Build three escalating findings with examples, visual changes and explicit payoffs. Keep uncertainty near the claim it qualifies. Condense affiliate context unless it answers the central question.
- R6.3 Replace exhaustive/universal claims with the actual scope. The original approximately 1,148-word draft is a starting point, not a locked runtime. Aim provisionally for 8–10 minutes only if the story earns it; time the spoken version.
- R6.4 Add a pauseable buyer card and description source map. Avoid a generic model roll call. End with what to check before buying and a specific invitation for the next useful research question.
- R6.5 Review separately as skeptical fact-checker, time-poor buyer and video editor. These are critical perspectives, not evidence from real users. Resolve substantive objections before narration.

Done when claims trace to evidence, visuals and spoken beats agree, and the script provides a useful answer without pretending Review Pirate tested the products firsthand.

### R11 — Measure and choose the next experiment

Dependencies: release and analytics access. Output: observations at 48 hours, 7 days and 28 days when available, then a postmortem.

- R11.1 Separate search, suggested and external traffic; report impressions, CTR and retention with denominators. Identify where viewers leave and whether the methodology earns attention.
- R11.2 Track subscribers, qualified comments, affiliate clicks, conversion and realized net revenue separately. Missing commerce data is unknown, not zero; distinguish pending commissions from cash received.
- R11.3 Change one major packaging/editorial variable at a time where practical. Low impressions mean insufficient evidence, not automatic rejection of the business.
- R11.4 Choose update, another robot-vacuum question, another category, or stop based on audience value and repeatability. The five-episode series is a hypothesis, not a compulsory spending commitment.

Done when observed results change a concrete next action. Future checks require a running session or an explicitly configured follow-up; this board does not schedule them.

## Research stopping and continuation

Initial research block: 3–4 active hours, then reassess evidence coverage and remaining uncertainty. This is a checkpoint, not a claim that deep research fits a fixed duration. Log effort rather than invent elapsed work. After covering the required strata, run two additional targeted search batches of up to ten screened results each against the most important unresolved conclusions. If either changes a buyer decision or central explanation, integrate it and continue the relevant investigation. If neither changes the decision and material claims are supported, close discovery with explicit residual gaps. Saturation within this bounded method is not internet-wide exhaustiveness.

If the block expires with major uncertainty, narrow the script to supported findings or report a justified additional research slice. Do not downgrade evidence standards to meet a same-day release target. Avoid expanding into unrelated products just to increase source count.

## Status and communication contract

For each task record: proposed / running / blocked / done; artifact links; verification; unresolved issue; next action. Keep this board current after each coherent work block. Use commits and focused PRs as checkpoints, not completion signals. Keep the executive brief short and link to deeper evidence. Use GitHub Issues only if assignment or asynchronous discussion adds value, and link back to the authoritative status instead of maintaining two boards.

Default execution is one integrating agent. Optional bounded delegation can be agreed for independent evidence searches or skeptical review; parallelism is not needed merely to make the plan look sophisticated. Final responsibility for deduplication, contradictions and the published conclusion stays with the integrator.
