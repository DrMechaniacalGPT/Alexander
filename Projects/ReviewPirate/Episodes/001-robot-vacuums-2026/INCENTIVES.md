# Incentives and Bias

bias
is not a red stamp

it is a variable
to investigate

## the first useful distinction

these are different

- reviewer got a free unit
- reviewer was paid to make a video
- reviewer earns an Amazon commission
- reviewer earns a direct-brand commission
- publisher runs display ads
- publication sells subscriptions
- creator has Patreon support
- reviewer personally earns more if product A sells
- company earns the same retailer rate whichever product sells

lumping all of that into

**biased**

would be lazy

## the interesting future analysis

## what we can say in episode 001

something like

"There's one more hidden variable:
how the review itself gets paid.

The public affiliate programs
aren't equal.
Roborock advertises 4 to 7 percent.
eufy advertises 7 to 15.
MOVA can go higher.
Amazon's standard Home rate is 3 percent.

That does **not** mean
a reviewer used those programs
or changed a verdict because of them.

So instead of calling people biased,
we log what we can actually verify:
who bought the unit,
who got a sample,
whether the video was sponsored,
and where the purchase links go.

Then we can ask the harder question
with data later."

that is enough

do not turn
episode one
into a prosecution
