## the point

the table explains
a surprising amount
of the disagreement

### candidate set

you cannot pick
a robot you have not tested

the newest reviewer
with the broadest current pool
has a structural advantage
in discovering a new winner

the oldest winner
is not necessarily wrong

it may simply be
the best surviving member
of an older test set

### objective function

there is no neutral
"overall"

one system
cares more about

pickup

another

mopping

another

price

another

local control

another

privacy

another

whether a preschooler
left a sock under the table

weights are conclusions

even when they look
like spreadsheet cells

### evidence age

a two-day review

a lab benchmark

a six-month owner report

and a reliability survey

answer different questions

Review Pirate should stop
letting them impersonate
one another
