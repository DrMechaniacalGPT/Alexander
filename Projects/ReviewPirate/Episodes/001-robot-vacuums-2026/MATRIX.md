# Evidence Matrix

this may be
the central visual language
of Review Pirate

one axis

products

the other

sources

the cells answer

**did this source actually review this product
and what did it conclude**

## why the matrix matters

two "best" lists
can disagree
before methodology
even enters the room

because
their candidate sets differ

the empty cells
are information

a reviewer
cannot select
a product
they did not test

the matrix makes
that invisible constraint
visible

## cell vocabulary

do not use
one red / green judgment

that would recreate
the problem
we are trying to fix

use two dimensions

### coverage

blank

not yet verified

dot

mentioned / contextual

filled cell

substantive review or test

thick border

deep or standardized testing

### direction

green

winner / strongly favorable

soft green

positive

amber

mixed / conditional

red

materially critical

grey

coverage exists
but no simple verdict
should be inferred

important

**blank does not mean bad**

it means

we have not verified
that this source
meaningfully covered
this product
