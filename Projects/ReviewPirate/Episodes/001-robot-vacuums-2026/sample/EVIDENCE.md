## Additional useful finding for the next episode pass

The Hook Up describes a high-pile carpet test using maximum suction and two passes. RTINGS' Curv 2 Flow carpet notes describe Balanced mode with Standard pathing and Carpet Boost. This is a documented protocol difference worth investigating. It does not by itself establish that settings cause the difference in results: debris, carpet, runs, sample units and measurement also differ. The sample does not present a fabricated causal explanation.

A separate refresh confirmed the current Vacuum Wars Top 20 identifies MOVA V70 Ultra Complete as its highest scorer. That fact was omitted from the sample because adding more winner names would lengthen the opening without improving this specific point.

## Source independence and public assets

RTINGS' guide and product reviews are one publisher/test ecosystem, not multiple independent confirmations. The Hook Up's article and video represent the same comparison, not two experiments. No owner-prevalence claims or affiliate rates appear in the sample. All product shapes are generic diagrams, not accurate model photography. No footage shows or simulates measured pickup.
