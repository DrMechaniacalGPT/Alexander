## Selected behavior, not permanent identity

A quick conditional recommendation precedes the methods. Original reviewers are visibly named, and the viewer can leave for their work. The rest of the sample earns attention by explaining a meaningful distinction. No logo sting or eight-product roll call precedes usefulness.

The sequence moves between a geometric floor illustration, a rug/dog visual joke, a handoff to sources, and a dark comparison scene. Motion signals meaning: the floor context changes, source claims occupy separate frames, and two distinct questions separate spatially. The moving vacuum is illustration only; debris is not removed to imply cleaning performance.

## Answer-first versus retention-first

Choose answer-first for the initial editorial experiment because it matches the owner's intended service to the viewer. We have not established that it maximizes watch time or revenue. A viewer leaving after getting a useful answer is not necessarily a product failure; distinguish that from an unclear or disappointing opening. Later compare retention, useful source referrals, returning viewers and qualified purchase questions. Do not hide a buying answer to manufacture retention.

The quick answer must remain conditional. A one-product command would be shorter but would contradict the evidence. The sample says “start by checking,” names the relevant household, and immediately provides the exception.

## Inspiration limits

The owner's Coffeezilla, Veritasium and GEICO examples suggest roles for recurring personality, explanatory motion and character memory. This pass did not conduct an episode-by-episode visual study of those channels or test their impact. Their popularity cannot establish which treatment will work for this project.

## Next visual experiment if this direction is too restrained

Use the same 10-second script in two cuts: this controlled motion style versus a cinematic illustrated room with a brief robot/animal/human host. Judge comprehension, personality, consistency, revision effort and whether the image implies fake testing. Keep the host to a small fraction of the runtime initially. Do not purchase an annual subscription to find out whether we even like the character.
