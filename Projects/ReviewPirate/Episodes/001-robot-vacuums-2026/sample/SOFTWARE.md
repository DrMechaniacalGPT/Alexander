## Higgsfield verification detail

The public page initially rendered no prices to text extraction. Opened its live UI, then switched billing from annual to monthly. It displayed $19/$59/$129. Did not click a purchase button or log in. The page stated that unlimited/free generations are website-only and not accessible through MCP/CLI, Canvas or Supercomputer. [Official credits explanation](https://higgsfield.ai/creator-hub/help-center/credits/how-credits-work) and [unlimited explanation](https://higgsfield.ai/blog/new-unlimited-more-models) should be rechecked before a purchase.

The displayed model table included approximately 8 credits/5 seconds for Kling 3.0 1080p and 45 credits/5 seconds for Seedance 2.0 1080p. As an illustrative budget, three 5-second shots × four attempts × 45 credits = 540 credits before reference images/other usage. That is twelve generated attempts, not twelve good usable shots. Settings and live per-generation quotes govern the actual cost. Quality, coherence and editability must be tested; a nominal clip allowance is not a finished-video allowance.
