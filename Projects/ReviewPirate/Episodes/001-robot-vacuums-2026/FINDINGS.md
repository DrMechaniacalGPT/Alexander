# Findings — Before Script

not a script

if this turns into a script too early
the research will start working
for the story
instead of the other way around

## finding 1

there is no shared definition
of "best robot vacuum"

that is not philosophy

it is visible
in the source design

Consumer Reports
currently separates
vacuum-only robots
from vacuum/mop combos

Vacuum Wars
ranks a huge current pool
with its own composite system

Expert Reviews
uses a standardized home
and visibly cares about value

The Hook Up
tests local control
alongside cleaning

these systems can disagree
without any of them
making a measurement error

## finding 2

the tested product set
may matter as much
as the scoring weights

Vacuum Wars
can put a brand-new MOVA V70
at the top
because it has already tested it

an older ranking system
cannot select
a product it has not tested

"best"
quietly means

best among the set
this source tested
under this method
at this date
in this market

that sentence
probably belongs
near the beginning
of the episode

## finding 3

rankings have a half-life

Vacuum Wars'
robot-vacuum hub
updated September 16
showed the Dreame X60 Max
at number one

its Top 20 page
updated September 24
showed MOVA V70
at number one

that is not necessarily
an inconsistency in method

it is a reminder
that a leaderboard
is a dated observation

Review Pirate should store

source
ranking
date

not just

source
ranking

## finding 4

price and region
are hidden objective functions

Expert Reviews
currently prefers the Eufy Omni E25
and explicitly praises
its price / performance

TechRadar explains
that the older Qrevo Curv
moved into its number-one slot
after its street price fell

the hardware did not change

the recommendation did

a US ranking
with different street prices
can rationally choose
something else

prices move faster
than review methodologies

a static score
can become wrong
without any performance changing

the T90 Pro Omni thread

then returns it in week two
is unusually illustrative

the video needs a section
on evidence age

hours
days
months
years

should not be blended together

## finding 6

obstacle avoidance
is not one test

a lab obstacle course
a clean adult apartment

and a house
with two small children
and rug fringe

are different distributions

this may explain
why a product can score well
in standardized obstacle testing
and still frustrate
a particular owner

## finding 7

advertised suction
is not measured suction

this is now concrete

Vacuum Wars compared

MOVA E40 Ultra
advertised around 19,000 Pa

against

MOVA P10 Pro Ultra
advertised around 13,000 Pa

their own sealed test measured

E40
0.88 kPa

P10 Pro
1.08 kPa

the lower advertised number
measured higher

another current Roborock
advertised at 36,000 Pa

measured below
the category average
in the Vacuum Wars bench test

while still cleaning well

so

manufacturer Pa
is not useless

but it is not
a cross-brand cleaning score

the episode can say that
with evidence

by explaining
which review systems
are actually informative
for which claims
