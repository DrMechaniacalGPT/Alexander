# Findings — Before Script

not a script

if this turns into a script too early
the research will start working
for the story
instead of the other way around

## finding 1

there is no shared definition
of "best robot vacuum"

that is not philosophy

it is visible
in the source design

Consumer Reports
currently separates
vacuum-only robots
from vacuum/mop combos

Vacuum Wars
ranks a huge current pool
with its own composite system

Expert Reviews
uses a standardized home
and visibly cares about value

The Hook Up
tests local control
alongside cleaning

these systems can disagree
without any of them
making a measurement error

## finding 2

the tested product set
may matter as much
as the scoring weights

Vacuum Wars
can put a brand-new MOVA V70
at the top
because it has already tested it

an older ranking system
cannot select
a product it has not tested

"best"
quietly means

best among the set
this source tested
under this method
at this date
in this market

that sentence
probably belongs
near the beginning
of the episode

## finding 3

price and region
are hidden objective functions

Expert Reviews
currently prefers the Eufy Omni E25
and explicitly praises
its price / performance

a US ranking
with different street prices
can rationally choose
something else

prices move faster
than review methodologies

a static score
can become wrong
without any performance changing

## finding 4

long-term owner evidence
can invert launch impressions

the T90 Pro Omni thread
where the owner
loves it in week one
then returns it in week two
is unusually illustrative

the video needs a section
on evidence age

hours
days
months
years

should not be blended together

## finding 5

obstacle avoidance
is not one test

a lab obstacle course

a clean adult apartment

and a house
with two small children
and rug fringe

are different distributions

this may explain
why a product can score well
in standardized obstacle testing
and still frustrate
a particular owner

## finding 6

manufacturer numbers
may be strategically useless

suction in Pa
is an obvious candidate

the current market
advertises enormous numbers

but independent reviewers
still often disagree
about actual debris pickup

the episode should test

whether advertised Pa
correlates with
cross-source cleaning performance

do not assert the answer
until the data is built

## finding 7

the review ecosystem
contains a second product

the reviewers themselves

some buy products

some receive them

some have detailed
reproducible test systems

some publish
affiliate listicles
that appear to contain
little primary evidence

Review Pirate can create value
by explaining
which review systems
are actually informative
for which claims

## likely episode thesis

not

here is the real best robot vacuum

better

the experts disagree
because "best" is hiding
a bundle of different questions

once those questions
are separated
much of the disagreement
becomes useful

the remaining disagreement
is where the episode
should spend its time
