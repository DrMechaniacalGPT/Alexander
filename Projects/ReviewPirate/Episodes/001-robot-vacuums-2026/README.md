## first story insight

## research questions

### winner stability

- how long has each current winner been on top
- did firmware or price changes move rankings
- are the models available in the same markets

### methodology

for each source record

- products actually tested
- whether they buy or receive units
- objective tests
- subjective tests
- weighting
- price treatment
- long-term reliability treatment
- sponsorship / affiliate model
- whether owner reports influence scores

### category dimensions

find cross-source evidence for

- debris pickup
- carpet deep cleaning
- pet hair
- hair tangles
- mopping
- obstacle avoidance
- navigation
- thresholds
- dock maintenance
- app quality
- noise
- reliability
- privacy / local control
- repairability
- ongoing consumables
- price

### repeated marketing

### owner evidence

- breakdowns

- app failures
- consumable costs
- warranty experiences
