# Omni selection recheck — decision, not a model catalogue

## Change the next test

**Begin with one Veo 3.1 Fast M02 character shot, then the same M02 brief in Omni 1.1. Do not commit to an Omni-only host/no-host edit before this gate.** Veo has the closest visually sampled reference: an expressive furred animal in a believable interior. Omni is a serious candidate because of relevant controls and lower iteration cost, but the research has not established that it produces our desired acting more reliably.

The previous Omni-first choice was primarily a cost/control decision: it fitted a complete short treatment comparison into fifty credits. That is a useful optimization only after visual capability clears the bar. Given the user's explicit rejection of cheaply adequate output, quality risk should determine the first generation.

This does not establish Veo as universally better. Its raccoon example is a selected vendor clip, the exact current Fast version is not proven by that gallery label, and audio was not assessed. The recommendation is an evidence-weighted test order, not a winner announcement.

## Revised fifty-credit gate

Prerequisites remain approved references, authorized access, one output per request, actual displayed quote verified, no hidden reference/voice spending.

1. Veo Fast, eight-second reference-based M02: **20 credits**. Same seated adult otter, room and line used in MOTION_TEST's reconciled version. No walk or complex hand action.
2. Omni 1.1, six-second M02 with identical references, words, framing and intended performance: **10 credits**. Compare the common useful duration; do not reward Veo simply for a longer clip.
3. Reserve **20 credits**, used only after review: either one Veo retry or two Omni retries. Revise a specific failed condition, not the whole artistic direction.

Total ceiling **50**. This gate answers whether either route can produce a credible host performance. It does **not** produce a fair host/no-host comparison, a complete 15-second cut or reliable model ranking. One successful sample can select the next experiment, not prove production yield.

Native voices can differ: record that as part of route quality, then use identical accepted audio for the later host/no-host editorial comparison. A listening reviewer remains necessary. If neither route clears the bar, switch to host-free motion tests instead of consuming more character retries blindly.

## Effect on the paid proposal

Flow Pro remains the first purchase candidate because both routes are in the same environment; tool choice does not force an Omni quality bet.

The earlier **230-credit** detailed plan applies if Omni wins: nine Omni clips (90), three Veo comparators (60), two optional repairs (80). If Veo wins, the mirrored plan costs **290**: nine Veo clips (180), three Omni comparators (30), two optional Omni edits (80). Both exclude reference/voice preparation and remain below the stated thousand-credit monthly allocation. Do not report 230 as a platform-independent ceiling.

At this stage, retain the first model-comparison attempts in the corresponding paid-plan counts rather than charging an additional duplicate set. A lower-cost accepted result is desirable, but visual and spoken quality is a pass/fail prerequisite.
