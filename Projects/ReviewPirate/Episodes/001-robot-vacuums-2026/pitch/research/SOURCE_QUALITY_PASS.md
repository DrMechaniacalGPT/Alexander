## What makes a source useful

The Hook Up makes a good explanatory reference because it exposes material/loading, settings, comparisons and scoring choices. The footage alternates labeled product demonstrations with readable result tables and highlighted rows. That makes the route from observation to verdict inspectable. Its historic cohort and weighting still constrain what its overall winner means. In-video price labels for some products differ from the written companion; verify price from the specific artifact used rather than silently merge them.

Vacuum Nerds' June awards mixes category recommendations with testing and home-use judgment, and explicitly discloses some supplied units and some purchases. Supplied hardware is relevant disclosure, not proof of bias. The auto-captions repeatedly name S7 MaxV while description identifies Saros 20: model names need visual/source verification before extraction. Its later V70 video shows a native leaderboard entry of 9.7 performance / 9.7 cleaning at 12:30, and weights at 12:20: cleaning 36%, navigation 28%, mopping 17%, usability 19%. Do not convert this into Vacuum Wars stars.

Vacuum Wars' accessible pages supply category results, reference averages and update history; their rankings are not a pure independent laboratory vote because owner ratings also influence them. Individual reviews, ranking updates and owner-score articles from this publisher share an evidence family.
