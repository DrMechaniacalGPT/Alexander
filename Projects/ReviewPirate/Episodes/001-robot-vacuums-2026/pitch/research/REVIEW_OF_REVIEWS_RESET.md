Our task is to reproduce the useful research journey of a person reading and watching reviews: discover the relevant products and sources; understand each source's comparisons, observations and conclusions; connect overlapping evidence; identify supported reasons to narrow the options. Synthesis comes after source reporting and remains distinguishable from it. We do not need to perform physical tests to synthesize evidence, but we must not imply first-hand authority or skip the reasoning.

## A substantive passage we can build, without inventing our own test

Start with the matrix and the models it introduces. Then compare the X60 accounts:

1. Present Tom's Guide's named surfaces, materials and reported pickup values, its mopping observations and its overall verdict.
2. Present The Hook Up's actual comparison field and its much less enthusiastic value judgment of the same named flagship. Explain its older-X50 alternative as that reviewer's recommendation, at its historical price difference.
3. Ask what can actually be reconciled: same SKU? same soils? comparable runs/settings? same available alternatives and prices? Its 40g mixture is not Tom's Guide's separate material tests.
4. Our inference is bounded: these sources provide different comparisons and purchase judgments. We have not established that weighting, price or methodology alone explains the difference. Complete the matched extraction before claiming a cause.

This is source content followed by synthesis. It gives the audience material they would otherwise need to collect from separate reviews. A generic injunction to choose according to one's floors does not provide the same service.

## How narrowing should work

Keep reviewer observations, reviewer verdicts and our synthesis in separate fields. A candidate can move off a viewer's path because it exceeds a stated budget, lacks a required feature, has a directly relevant disadvantage against an alternative, or has a superseded recommendation with a demonstrated reason. Every move must retain the source and assumption.

A low score in one dimension is not proof of overall inferiority. An older date alone is not grounds to discard a reviewer. A newer model with a similar name is not automatically an upgrade. Conflicting evidence can prevent elimination. Do not pre-script eight becoming two and then search for justification.
