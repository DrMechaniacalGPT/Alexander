September 26, 2026. Owner authorized autonomous execution through the next listening pitch. This supersedes prior buying-guide scripts and production task ordering. The previous listening format is useful; its narration is rejected. Do not animate that narration.

## Product promise

A short, visually engaging guide to the review landscape: what credible reviewers found, which findings change the shortlist, and which original reviews viewers should watch for depth. Our contribution is connecting their work. Never imply we physically tested these vacuums. Keep reported findings, reviewer recommendations and our inferences distinguishable.

Around one minute is a starting target for the entire film, not an introductory summary followed by a long guide. Let consequential findings determine length. No fixed number of beats, final candidates or predetermined winner. Referral, finding and decision develop together throughout the film.

## Execution and acceptance

1. **Recover and widen discovery.** Recover the existing source list and audience research. Search broader review/video/model landscape with explicit market and date. Deduplicate videos, companions and later guides sharing tests. Record found, screened and examined-in-depth counts separately. Never turn search hits into a claim of watched videos. Views/likes measure reach, not quality.
2. **Evaluate original reviews.** Compare credible comparative videos and specialist reviews. Inspect accessible video frames and captions; record timestamps actually observed and sampling limits. Assess disclosed protocols, comparable conditions, measurements, failures/corrections, conclusions, current candidate coverage and usefulness of presentation. The Hook Up is a candidate, not a selected winner. Choose a starting review only if justified; choose specialist referrals independently.
3. **Build the evidence matrix.** Preserve exact source identity, date, model/package, native score/rank/verdict, candidate set, method and locators. Do not fabricate a common scale. Not reviewed, inaccessible, and not yet verified are different states. Retain recommendation, coverage and measurement layers separately.
4. **Add owner experience.** Amazon ratings/counts and Reddit owner reports belong in a distinct evidence layer. Record exact variants, dates, selection and deduplication; seek contrary accounts. A compact status icon needs a documented meaning; it is not an invented Reddit score or reliability rate. Only promote consequential supported findings into narration.
5. **Investigate price changes.** Preserve the price assumption at review time and compare equivalent current offers. Seek cases where a price-driven preference reverses. Verify that the source actually preferred B's relevant performance before inferring B now offers better value at equal prices. Distinguish durable changes from temporary offers. Price-based conclusions should state their dependency.
6. **Explore complete stories.** Develop several genuinely different paths from opening to ending. Possibilities include an unusually informative review as the starting framework, a cross-source consensus, a meaningful contradiction, a supported dominance/value reduction. These are hypotheses, not findings. At every step record what the viewer already knows, cells in focus, what changes, why it matters and the question that makes the next step necessary. Do not generate disconnected chunks and merely permute them.
7. **Challenge the provisional choice.** Check sources outside the central matrix and overlooked models for counter-evidence, ownership issues and contradictory results. Let this change both conclusion and script. Incorporate important contrary evidence immediately whenever found. Keep a record of challenges and disposition.
8. **Compare listening pitches.** Produce a small set of complete concise drafts, each with scene stills and individually playable neutral narration. Evaluate the viewer experience continuously: clarity, source-earned trust, visible progress, visual interest and cognitive load. Delete beats that change nothing. Recommend a direction with substantive tradeoffs, not an arbitrary score.
9. **Refine approved art and prove production.** Keep the photoreal otter, house, font and grid style. Enlarge the vacuum and name robot vacuums in the thumbnail. Use exact review thumbnails/article identities and accurate product images on axes when accessible. First frame matches thumbnail; otter acts, board moves, richer evidence appears. Keep persistent board state and show meaningful otter actions. Motion production needs actual model access and quality evidence; stills alone do not prove animation.
10. **Checkpoint and verify.** Save source-backed findings, data and rationale as reviewable GitHub changes. Keep generated media local unless authorized otherwise. Refresh dated claims before release. Publishing is not part of this approval.

## Review experience

The grid is the subject, with clusters highlighted and explained. Each zoom should earn an intelligible change: narrow options, reveal a genuine fork, qualify a recommendation, or identify the right reviewer for a concern. Gray a product only with a supported reason and visible conditions. Do not remove an entire reviewer because of one old review, affiliation, or disagreement. Never manufacture progress using a temporary stock outage.

Start by testing which opening produces the strongest useful progress. Eight distinct lead picks do not establish eight conflicting experiments: dates, models, markets and award scopes differ. Explain that distinction through useful findings, not a long methodological preamble.
