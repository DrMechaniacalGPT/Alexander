For premium convenience on mostly hard floors, compare the Saros 10R. For budget vacuuming, look at the Q7 M5—if you'll clear clutter and empty its bin. These are starting points from the reviews we checked. Embedded pet hair needs a closer look.

First: what needs cleaning?

The Saros 10R earns its place for navigation and everyday convenience. Carpet hair is where the evidence gets harder to read. Vacuum Wars reports about ninety-two percent pickup in its flattened carpet-hair test. Another lab, RTINGS, describes disappointing pickup of embedded hair. Those are different tests of the same vacuum.

We can't turn them into one meaningful average. Different carpets, hair and settings may matter, but we haven't established what caused the gap. If embedded fur is your main problem, an overall winner badge isn't enough. I've linked the actual tests so you can see what each one measured.

Second: what could stop it?

In The Hook Up's April seven-model comparison, the Curv 2 Flow wins overall. Carpet vacuuming and hard-floor mopping count double. But in that same comparison, it scores two out of six for object avoidance.

If you keep the floor clear, that tradeoff may work. If cables and toys regularly land on the carpet, that weakness may matter more than the award.

Even the mopping evidence is mixed: Vacuum Wars found weak dried-stain cleaning. So this award alone doesn't settle the mop question either. The comparison details are linked below.

Third: what work will you still do?

The Curv 2 Flow manual calls for cleaning the dirty-water tank, caring for the filter and wiping sensors. The dock saves work. It doesn't remove every chore.

I've linked the manual's maintenance section. Before paying for automation, look at the parts you'll still be handling—and whether the dock fits where you want it.

That's also why a simpler machine can make sense. The Q7 M5 is a reasonable basic-vacuuming starting point if floor preparation and manual emptying are acceptable. The Plus version adds a self-emptying dock.

So: premium hard-floor convenience, start with the Saros. Basic pickup with more work from you, compare the Q7 M5. For embedded carpet hair, use the specific tests instead of a blanket recommendation.
