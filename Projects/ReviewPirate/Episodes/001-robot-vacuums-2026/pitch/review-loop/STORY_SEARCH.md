## Why the chosen sequence is ordered this way

The fur finding is simple enough to understand before learning testing terminology. It establishes immediately that an overall award does not settle a household-specific need. The next model shares a confusing family name, so the narration explicitly distinguishes it, then shows why a positive mopping result cannot travel unqualified across different stain protocols. The threshold beat stays with that same Flow model and supplies a same-test comparison with X60: this is a resolution to the preceding uncertainty, not a new unconnected premium option. The ending preserves three branches and leaves four original products unassessed.

The draft's scope is deliberately not 'which vacuum should everyone buy.' It is a short source-navigation and comparison guide. A wider purchasing recommendation would require more comparable price, ownership, competitor and method evidence. This is not a reason to fabricate elimination of the remaining columns.
