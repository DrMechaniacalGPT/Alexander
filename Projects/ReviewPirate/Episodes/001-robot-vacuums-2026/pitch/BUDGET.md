## Full episode: scenario, not quote

Assume 15 accepted generated shots, each contributing about 8 edited seconds, for 120 seconds of cinematic footage. The rest of a roughly 3-minute edit consists of purposeful evidence inserts, accurate product imagery, maintenance explanation and source/decision cards. This is a planning mix, not an optimal retention ratio or a commitment to a particular runtime.

At current Omni 720p eight-second pricing of 12 credits, three attempts per accepted shot would cost 15 × 3 × 12 = 540 credits. Eight attempts would cost1,440. Add reference preparation, repairs, voice and any Veo comparators separately. This excludes trimming losses: if a nominal eight-second clip yields only four usable seconds, twice as many accepted clips may be needed. A prettier model can still cost more per useful second through failures.

For VeoFast reference generation at 20 credits per eight-second output, the same simplified attempt assumptions produce 900–2,400 credits before other work. These are sensitivity calculations, not forecasts. A successful short test must supply our actual yield before an episode budget is credible.

Subscription cash cost and credits consumed are different measures. A paid month costs the full subscription even if we consume a fraction of its credits. Track agent effort, owner effort, edit time, retries, accepted seconds and cash separately. Do not report local compute or labor as economically free.

## Purchase decision, when needed

The proposal should name the exact service, month-to-month plan, price at checkout, test allocation and stopping rule. No annual commitment, automatic top-up or extra subscription is implied. If the account already includes usable credits, begin there. If the first shots fail, diagnose the performance, reference or scene design before upgrading plans.
