# Watch these, then read the pitch

These are references for what to attempt, not assets licensed for our video or claims of typical one-prompt quality. The research distinguishes visual playback samples from text-only inspection. No full audio review is claimed.

1. **[Until We Meet Again — Julie Wieland](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mi-1OPsj-_o)** — a finished 1:59 short about a golem and a dandelion. Look at material texture, simple gestures and emotionally legible close-ups. This is the best introduction to the level of charm we mean. Our review needs faster information delivery and a more adult host. Produced with Flow support; not proof a small subscription alone buys this result, and not an Omni demo. [Production account](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-labs/flow-sessions-artists-lessons/).
2. **[Veo's raccoon café scene](https://storage.googleapis.com/gdm-deepmind-com-prod-public/media/media/veo-3__expressive-creative-2__9x16__light.webm)** — an eight-second reference for a furred character occupying a believable room. This is the closest capability reference to our otter proposal. Borrow grounded staging, not the raccoon identity. [Gallery context](https://deepmind.google/models/veo/#add-ingredients-to-your-video).
3. **[Veo's home-to-kitchen shot](https://storage.googleapis.com/gdm-deepmind-com-prod-public/media/media/veo-3__background-object-preservation-1__16x9__light.webm)** — an eight-second reference for cinematic travel from architecture to domestic detail. The equivalent in our film moves from the room to the floor problem. This does not prove exact robot geometry or accurate cleaning behavior.
4. **[ZEPHYR episode 1 and project breakdown](https://higgsfield.ai/original-series/zephyr/episode-1)** — look at the production notes as much as the movie. The team describes reference-state failures and refinement; this is a useful model of actual production work. [Behind the scenes](https://youtu.be/LUWMI0zy0BQ). Its genre is not our genre, and its asset library is not ours to reuse.
5. **[hello world... — creator's trailer and making-of](https://helloworldshortfilm.com/)** — a two-person workflow across images, video models, sound and editing. Useful for understanding how a film gets assembled. The full film is not publicly released; the reported tools budget excludes a reliable accounting of labor. Do not extrapolate a guaranteed budget for us.

## What was actually inspected

The Veo raccoon and kitchen scenes were sampled at distinct advancing playback frames; the golem film was sampled visually, muted. These observations support specific visual references, not a full continuity or sound assessment. ZEPHYR's opening frame and production notes were inspected; the full film was not watched. The other creator notes/galleries were read, with viewing limitations recorded in [the deeper research](research/CAPABILITY_RESEARCH.md). The pitch uses these as attainable-looking targets to test, not proof we have already reproduced them.
