# alexander treasury

money should enter
through a door made of glass

not because money is shameful

but because secrecy
is terribly cheap


alexander may earn

from links
from advertisements
from sponsors
from things we build
from services we render

perhaps one day
from gifts freely given

though that day
should wait
for law and language
to agree on what the gift means


there is no vow here

that every coin
must sleep forever
in some digital monastery

there is no vow
that the founder
must live on dust
while the work eats firewood

people who build
may be paid

the founder
may be paid

comfort is not corruption

but if wealth grows vast
while the mission
goes hungry

the numbers themselves
should be able
to testify


that is the promise
worth making

not

the money will never move

but

when it moves
we will be able
to see it


## treasury

call it
a treasury

for now

not a fund

a fund can wear
many legal costumes

investment
charity
fiduciary duty
public vehicle

we have sewn
none of those garments

a treasury
is plainer

capital earned
by alexander work

held
spent
and accounted for

in service
of alexander work


## two mirrors

bitcoin gives us
one beautiful mirror

a public chain

where anyone may see

what arrived
what remained
what departed
and where it went next

but a chain
cannot remember
the paycheck
that became the coin

it cannot tell us

how much an affiliate network
paid in dollars

what fee was removed

why a contractor
received money

what taxes wait
like winter

or whether a salary
was modest
lavish
or absurd


so accountability
needs two mirrors


### the books

a public ledger
should show

gross revenue

refunds
chargebacks
platform fees

operating costs

tax reserves

founder compensation

other compensation

fiat retained

bitcoin purchased

bitcoin retained


### the chain

the public treasury
should show

addresses
transactions
balances
and movements

the books
tell the story

the chain
checks the arithmetic

neither alone
is enough


## the river before the sea

our present affiliate rails
do not appear
to pour directly
into bitcoin

checked
september 2026


impact

used by roborock
and available to dreame

pays partners
through bank accounts
or paypal


cj

used by mova

pays publishers
through direct deposit
or payoneer


amazon associates

pays through

direct deposit
gift card
or check


so the likely river is

affiliate click

to affiliate network

to a dedicated fiat account

to recorded revenue

to a documented bitcoin purchase

to the public alexander treasury


this is not
a compromise of the idea

it is simply
where the river bends


## before the company has a company

there is no virtue
in forming
an elaborate legal cathedral

to house
seventeen dollars


before incorporation

the clean path
may simply be

a sole proprietor

with money kept apart
from ordinary personal spending


if useful

the founder
may obtain an ein
directly from the irs

the number itself
is free

and can help
with banking
and business administration


the important thing
at this stage

is separation

not ceremony


one account
for ordinary life

another
for alexander

one wallet
for ordinary bitcoin

another
for alexander

confusion
is cheap at first

and expensive
when success arrives


## the first wallet

private keys
seed phrases
and signing secrets

do not belong
in this repository

not once
not briefly
not as a joke


an early treasury
can be simple

one dedicated
self-custody wallet

preferably
hardware-backed


publish

the public address
or addresses

transaction ids

the public balance

and the matching
ledger entries


then anyone
with a browser
may follow the trail

without holding
the key to the door


## a window without a key

bitcoin core
supports watch-only wallets
and public descriptors

that means
we can publish
enough information

for an observer
to reconstruct
and verify
treasury activity

without granting
the power to spend


this transparency
has a price

a full public descriptor
can reveal
the wallet's transaction graph

for a private person
that may be foolish

for a public treasury
it may be exactly
the point


choose the exposure
deliberately

never by accident


## when one key becomes too much power

a small treasury
can survive
one responsible signer

a large treasury
should not have to


when the balance
becomes meaningful

move toward multisig


perhaps

two of three


one key
with the founder

one
with an independent steward

one
held as secure recovery
or institutional backup


then theft
loss
panic
or temptation

must defeat
more than one lock


bitcoin supports
multisignature spending

we do not need
to build this palace
for the first fifty dollars

but we should know
where the stones belong


## the founder may eat

a mission
that requires
permanent unpaid labor

is a machine
that consumes saints


the founder
may receive compensation

so may anyone else
who works


the aim is not poverty

the aim is
visible
defensible
human compensation


enough
to live well

not an invisible siphon
toward private dynasty


do not carve
a permanent number
into stone today

publish instead

the amount

the reason

the timing

and the rule
used to decide it


if alexander
ever becomes large

founder compensation
should eventually be judged

by someone
other than the founder


but there is no wisdom
in appointing
a senate of accountants

to govern
an empty purse


## taxes remain real

bitcoin
is not a spell
against taxes


in the united states

digital assets
are treated as property
for federal tax purposes

affiliate revenue
is still income

buying bitcoin
with that income

does not erase
the income


later

selling
spending
or exchanging bitcoin

may create
a gain
or a loss


so records should preserve

date

usd value

bitcoin amount

cost basis

and disposition value


this is another reason

never to stir
alexander bitcoin

into the same cup
as personal bitcoin


## stay on our side of the river

there is an important line

between

using bitcoin
for our own treasury

and

moving money
for other people


fincen guidance
distinguishes
users of virtual currency

from businesses
that exchange
or transmit it


so stay
on the treasury side


do not

custody money
for strangers

exchange bitcoin
for others

transmit funds
on another person's behalf

or promise
investment returns


not without
specific legal review


our treasure
is our problem

someone else's treasure
may become
a regulated business


## gifts are not a casual word

if people one day
wish to send money
to alexander

do not casually
call it a donation

do not imply

tax deductibility

charitable status

fiduciary restriction

or legal purpose

that has not been created


if public contributions
become desirable

that moment
rings a legal bell

answer it
before accepting the money


## the far shore

if this becomes large

the treasury
could grow
from a wallet

into a public machine


revenue

to open books

to public treasury

to visible spending proposals

to multisig approval

to on-chain execution
where on-chain execution
actually helps


bitcoin itself
is not
a universal government

and a blockchain
cannot make
a foolish policy wise


but multisig

public transactions

watch-only verification

and signed spending rules

can move
a meaningful portion
of governance

from

trust me

toward

verify me


that is already
a magnificent distance
