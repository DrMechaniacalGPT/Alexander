# Alexander Treasury

the point is accountability

not a promise
that money can never move
or that the founder
can never be paid

Alexander may earn money

from

- affiliate revenue
- advertising
- sponsorship
- products
- services
- donations
  if that is ever legally reviewed
  and deliberately enabled

the intention

is to use the economic power
created by Alexander projects

to build more things

some money
may pay the people
doing the work

including the founder

that is allowed

the point is not
performative poverty

the point is

do not quietly turn
a mission
into private generational wealth

if compensation exists

make it visible

if spending exists

make it visible

if the policy changes

make the change visible

accountability
before promises


## call it a treasury

for now

not a fund

fund can imply

investment management
charitable structure
or obligations
that do not exist

Alexander Treasury

means

capital earned
or received
by Alexander activity

held
spent
and reported
for Alexander


## what the blockchain can prove

a public Bitcoin treasury
can make

- treasury receipts
- treasury balances
- treasury transfers
- treasury spending transactions

independently observable

that is useful

but

the blockchain cannot prove

- how much affiliate revenue
  was earned before conversion
- whether a bank fee existed
- why a payment was made
- what a contractor did
- what taxes are owed
- whether a salary was reasonable

so full accountability
requires two layers

### public books

a simple ledger

showing

- gross revenue
- reversals / chargebacks
- platform fees
- operating costs
- taxes reserved
- founder compensation
- other compensation
- Bitcoin purchases
- fiat held
- Bitcoin held

### public treasury

the Bitcoin transactions
that correspond
to the ledger

the books explain
the chain

the chain verifies
the books

## Bitcoin treasury design

do not put
private keys
seed phrases
or signing material
in this repository

ever

### early stage

one dedicated
self-custody wallet

preferably
hardware-backed

publish

- treasury address / addresses
- transaction IDs
- public balance view
- corresponding ledger entries

### better transparency

Bitcoin Core supports
watch-only wallets
and public descriptors

a public watch-only view
can let anyone
verify treasury activity

without
giving them
spending authority

publishing
a full watch-only descriptor
also exposes
the wallet's transaction graph

for a treasury
that may be desirable

but it should be
a deliberate transparency choice

### later stage

when the treasury
becomes meaningful

move to multisig

example

2 of 3

founder

independent steward

backup / institutional key

then

one person
cannot silently move
the entire treasury

Bitcoin supports
multisignature addresses

this is a natural
future control

not something
we need
for the first $50


## compensation

the founder
may be paid

that is not
a contradiction

a project
that requires
permanent unpaid labor
is not durable

the intended norm

is

comfortable
visible
defensible compensation

not

extract
as much private wealth
as legally possible

do not define
a permanent number today

instead

publish

- amount
- purpose
- timing
- policy used

if Alexander
becomes large enough

founder compensation
should eventually
be approved
by someone
other than the founder

but

do not invent
a board
to govern
zero dollars


## taxes

Bitcoin does not
make taxes disappear

in the United States

digital assets
are treated as property
for federal tax purposes

affiliate income
is still business income

buying Bitcoin
with that income
does not erase
the original income

later

selling
spending
or exchanging Bitcoin
can create
taxable gain or loss

records must preserve

- date
- USD value
- Bitcoin amount
- basis
- disposition value

that accounting
is another reason
not to casually
mix personal Bitcoin
with Alexander Bitcoin


## regulatory boundary

holding
buying
and spending
Bitcoin
for Alexander's own account

is very different
from

operating an exchange
or transmitting money
for other people

FinCEN guidance
distinguishes
users of virtual currency
from businesses
that exchange
or transmit it

stay on
the treasury side

do not

- custody money for strangers
- exchange Bitcoin for others
- transmit money on behalf of others
- promise investment returns

without
specific legal review


## donations

do not casually
call incoming money
a donation

or imply

tax deductibility
charitable status
or fiduciary restrictions

unless those things
actually exist

if Alexander
later wants
public contributions

that is a trigger
for legal review

not a sentence
to add to a website


## the end state

if this becomes large

the interesting system is

revenue

-> public books

-> public treasury

-> transparent spending proposals

-> multisig approval

-> on-chain execution
where useful

Bitcoin itself
does not execute
arbitrary governance logic

but

multisig
public transactions
watch-only verification
and signed spending policies

can move
a meaningful amount
of governance
from

trust me

to

verify me

that is enough
for a strong first architecture
