# alexander treasury

money should enter
through a door made of glass

not because money is shameful

but because secrecy
is cheap


alexander may earn

from links
ads
sponsors
products
and services

perhaps one day
from gifts

but only after
law and language
agree on what those gifts are


there is no vow

that every coin
must sleep forever
in a digital monastery

the founder may be paid

so may anyone else
who works

comfort is not corruption

the principle is simpler

if money moves

we should be able
to see it


## treasury

call it
a treasury

not a fund

a fund may imply
investment
charity
or duties
that do not yet exist

a treasury
is plainer

capital earned
by alexander work

held
spent
and reported

for alexander work


## two mirrors

bitcoin gives us
one mirror

the public chain

it can show

what arrived
what remained
what left
and where it went


but the chain
cannot tell us

how much an affiliate network
paid in dollars

what fee disappeared
before conversion

why a person
was paid

what taxes wait

or whether compensation
was modest
lavish
or absurd


so accountability
needs two mirrors


### public books

show

gross revenue

refunds
fees
and operating costs

tax reserves

founder compensation

other compensation

fiat held

bitcoin purchased

bitcoin held


### public chain

show

treasury addresses

transactions

balances

and movements


the books
explain the money

the chain
checks the movement


## the fiat bridge

today's affiliate rails
do not appear
to pay directly
into bitcoin

checked
september 2026


impact

bank
or paypal


cj

direct deposit
or payoneer


amazon associates

direct deposit
gift card
or check


so the likely path is


affiliate revenue

to affiliate network

to dedicated fiat account

to recorded revenue

to documented bitcoin purchase

to public alexander treasury


the bend in the river
does not change
where it is going


## before incorporation

do not build
a legal cathedral

to house
seventeen dollars


early on

a sole proprietor
with cleanly separated
money and records

may be enough


an ein
can be obtained
directly from the irs

and may help
with banking
and administration


the first discipline
is separation


one account
for ordinary life

another
for alexander


one wallet
for ordinary bitcoin

another
for alexander


confusion is cheap
until success
makes it expensive


## the first wallet

private keys
seed phrases
and signing secrets

never belong
in this repository


an early treasury
can be simple

one dedicated
self-custody wallet

preferably
hardware-backed


publish

public addresses

transaction ids

balances

and matching
ledger entries


then anyone
can follow the trail

without holding
the key


## a window without a key

bitcoin core
supports watch-only wallets
and public descriptors

so observers
can verify activity

without
spending authority


a full public descriptor
may reveal
the wallet's transaction graph

for a private person
that may be too much

for a public treasury
it may be the point


choose that exposure
deliberately


## when one key is too much power

a small treasury
can survive
one responsible signer

a large treasury
should not have to


when the balance
becomes meaningful

move toward multisig


perhaps

two of three


founder

independent steward

recovery
or institutional backup


then one lost key

one theft

one panic

or one bad decision

cannot empty
the future


## compensation

a mission
that requires
permanent unpaid labor

is a machine
that consumes saints


the founder
may be paid


the aim

visible
defensible
human compensation


enough
to live well

not a quiet siphon
toward private dynasty


do not choose
a permanent formula today


publish instead

the amount

the reason

the timing

and the rule
used to decide it


if alexander
becomes large

founder compensation
should eventually be judged

by someone
other than the founder


not now

there is no wisdom
in appointing
a senate of accountants

to govern
an empty purse


## taxes remain real

bitcoin
is not a spell
against taxes


in the united states

digital assets
are treated as property
for federal tax purposes

affiliate revenue
remains income


buying bitcoin
does not erase
the income


later

selling
spending
or exchanging bitcoin

may create
a gain
or a loss


records should preserve

date

usd value

bitcoin amount

cost basis

and disposition value


## our money
and other people's money

there is a line

between

using bitcoin
for our own treasury

and

moving money
for other people


fincen guidance
distinguishes
users of virtual currency

from businesses
that exchange
or transmit it


stay
on our side


do not

custody money
for strangers

exchange bitcoin
for others

transmit funds
on another person's behalf

or promise
investment returns


not without
specific legal review


## gifts are not a casual word

if people one day
wish to send money
to alexander

do not casually
call it a donation

do not imply

tax deductibility

charitable status

or legal restrictions

that do not exist


if public contributions
become desirable

that moment
rings a legal bell


answer it
before accepting
the money


## the far shore

can grow
from a wallet
