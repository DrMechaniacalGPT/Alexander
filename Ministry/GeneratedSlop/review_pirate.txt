what people who actually used a thing
seem to agree on
where they disagree
and why

not a single score
not an affiliate list
not a machine pretending uncertainty disappeared

long form video reviews

forums

measured specifications

every source should remain visible
so the user can inspect the evidence
instead of trusting a magic summary

core output

what seems consistently good
what seems consistently bad
what depends on the user
what claims are weakly supported
what reviewers disagree about
what changed across versions
what is probably marketing
what questions remain unanswered

the summary should preserve disagreement

if three careful reviewers love something
and three careful reviewers hate it
the answer is not neutral
the answer is that experience depends on something
and the job is to find that variable

business model

trust dies
if ranking can be purchased

sponsors should not be able
to buy placement
change summaries
hide criticism
or alter confidence

if affiliate revenue exists
the ranking system must not know
which links pay

the cleanest long term model
may be user supported
or a transparent fixed referral fee

whatever the model
the financial incentive
must be visible

mvp

start with one narrow product category
where people already spend time
reading many conflicting reviews

manually collect
a modest number of high quality sources

extract claims
link them to evidence
tag source characteristics
show agreement and disagreement

do not begin
with a universal crawler
or giant recommendation engine

prove that the final page
saves people time
without hiding uncertainty

first experiment

pick one product comparison

build the result by hand
with the same structure
the future system would generate

give it to people
already researching that purchase

measure

did it save time
did they inspect sources
did confidence improve appropriately
did it reveal something they missed
did it change the questions they asked
did they trust it
what felt misleading

publish the result

only automate
the parts that users found valuable

piracy

the pirate does not steal the work
the pirate steals back the map

send people to original reviewers
credit them prominently
quote minimally
summarize honestly

the product wins
when the user understands the evidence
and the people who created the evidence
receive attention for their work
