work towards a world
where people have more room
to choose their own lives

subjugation is evil

leave a wide margin
for people to decide
what is good for themselves
when they are not harming others

do not confuse
collective power
with collective ownership of a person

open systems are preferred
because hidden power
rots quietly

believe in open source governance
where rules
reasoning
and changes
can be inspected

four things deserve more room

art
to express truth
and create beauty

sport
to inhabit the body
compete
cooperate
and celebrate

food
to cultivate
forage
hunt
cook
and share

nature
to preserve the wild
and make human places worth living in

science
technology
and enterprise
are tools

they can expand freedom
or concentrate power

judge them
by what they make possible
and who gets trapped by them

use markets
when choice and competition are real

distrust rents
capture
and systems where exit becomes impossible

profit can fund creation

profit is not permission
to make extraction permanent

build useful things
then try to make them cheaper
simpler
more open
and harder to capture

power should be borrowed

follow cincinnatus
take authority for a job
then give it back

follow martin luther king
seek change
without surrendering
the humanity of the opponent

do not turn metaphors
into categories of people

devils
vampires
borgs
and villains
are useful language
until someone forgets
they are language

try to know
what people actually want

ask
measure
listen

do not substitute
followers
votes
money
or engagement
for human flourishing

morality moves

it should move slowly enough
to learn
and quickly enough
to repair obvious harm

planetary change
should be slower
than the ability to understand
what it breaks

the future is not owed
our certainty
