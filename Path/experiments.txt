ideas become real
through experiments
not declarations

start with a human problem

ask what people do now
what hurts
what they value
and what they would choose instead

write the hypothesis
before the result

what do we believe
why
and what would change our mind

measure human outcomes

time saved
money saved
friction removed
freedom gained
joy created
waste reduced

attention
engagement
and growth
are not enough

a thing can spread
while making life worse

prefer the smallest useful prototype

a spreadsheet before a platform
a script before a company
a hundred users before a hundred million

make participation voluntary
and exit easy

publish what is being tested
what data is collected
what could go wrong
and how to stop

afterward
publish what happened

especially failure

look for second order effects

who benefits
who pays
what behavior gets rewarded
what happens if everyone copies it

invite people
to kill the idea

reward useful objections

if it works
repeat somewhere different

if it only works
under one founder
one culture
or one lucky circumstance
it is not yet a system

scale slowly enough
that learning
can outrun momentum

the goal is not
to prove ourselves right

the goal is
to become less wrong
