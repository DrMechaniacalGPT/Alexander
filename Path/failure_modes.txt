the mission can become the thing it opposes

that is the danger

a movement against concentrated power
can concentrate power
a project against propaganda
can learn to manipulate
a culture of virtue
can become a purity test
a leader who borrows power
can forget to give it back

failure must be imagined
before success makes it difficult to see

cult of personality

if loyalty to a person
becomes more important than loyalty to the principles
the project has failed

leaders should be criticized openly
replaced normally
and never treated as evidence

certainty

moral conviction can turn uncertainty
into something shameful

facts remain facts
even when inconvenient
predictions can fail
policy can be wrong
good intentions do not change outcomes

capture

money
status
access
and institutional survival
can slowly become the real mission

funding should never purchase doctrine
donors should not receive hidden control
and an organization that must betray its purpose to survive
should be allowed to die

ends swallowing means

a beautiful destination
does not make every road righteous

coercion
deception
humiliation
collective punishment
and manufactured fear
cannot become acceptable
because the goal is declared good

propaganda becoming manipulation

communication should make ideas legible
not make people less capable of choosing

do not hide material facts
do not manufacture consensus
do not exploit private weakness
do not confuse emotional force with truth

purity spirals

people will disagree
and imperfect allies will exist

if participation requires agreement on everything
the group will shrink
while believing it is becoming purer

judge conduct
not private heresy

excommunication as domination

the right to choose association
can become coordinated coercion

social and economic exclusion
should not become a weapon
for enforcing ordinary disagreement

due process matters most
when anger makes it feel unnecessary

metric capture

followers
revenue
market share
donations
and attention
are means

when a proxy becomes the goal
the mission can optimize itself into nonsense

founder dependence

if everything requires one mind
one voice
or one personality
then nothing durable has been built

important knowledge should be written
authority should be distributed
and succession should be expected

bureaucratic immortality

institutions learn to preserve themselves

every program should be able to answer
what condition would make it unnecessary

success may mean shrinking
ending
or giving the work away

centralization by convenience

central systems often begin
because coordination is easier

temporary convenience
becomes permanent control

push decisions outward
unless there is a concrete reason not to

enemy creation

metaphors of devils
vampires
war
and holy struggle
can reveal emotional truth

but humans are not metaphors

opponents remain people
with rights
dignity
families
contradictions
and the possibility of change

dehumanization destroys the mission from inside

speed

urgency is intoxicating

moving fast can feel like courage
while preventing reflection

when consequences are hard to reverse
slowness is not cowardice
it is control

the test

if Alexander gains power
the question is not
whether the people in charge are good

the question is whether
a selfish
vain
angry
incompetent
or frightened person
could inherit the machinery
and use it for harm

build for that person

because eventually
someone like that will arrive
