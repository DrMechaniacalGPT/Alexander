details

1 build

start with a real problem
and the smallest thing
that can test whether anyone cares

2 sell

money is unusually honest feedback

free usage can mean
love
curiosity
or boredom with a credit card safely hidden

ask for value
when value is being created

3 learn

watch what people do
not what the roadmap says

publish failures internally
before success teaches everyone to lie

4 reinvest

fund the next improvement
and the next project

do not raise money
merely to become good at raising money

5 delegate

hire people
who can own a whole problem

give them room

central approval
is not a personality trait
worth scaling

6 repeat

multiple small projects
can run in parallel

kill weak ones
feed strong ones
steal lessons from both

7 let mature things become boring

when a product is stable
reduce friction

lower cost
lower maintenance
lower ceremony

sometimes success means
a smaller team
less margin
open source
a standard
or a graceful death

organizations are tools

they are not pets
