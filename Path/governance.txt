power is borrowed
never owned

legitimacy comes from voluntary participation

no one should be forced to believe
and sincere disagreement
is not treason

truth comes before certainty

important claims should have evidence
assumptions should be named
predictions should be allowed to fail

dissent is a feature

hear the strongest objection
before a decision
becomes hard to reverse

decisions should be made
as close as practical
to the people affected

centralize coordination
only when the smaller units
cannot do the job well

prefer reversible experiments
to irreversible declarations

test small
measure
publish what happened
keep what works

never use fear
deception
or coercion
as a shortcut to agreement

attention is not legitimacy
popularity is not truth

due process matters
most when anger
makes it feel unnecessary

state the accusation
show the evidence
allow a response
make appeal possible

open the books
open the rules
open the reasoning

disclose conflicts

leaders are temporary stewards

succession should be ordinary
not a constitutional emergency

judge the system
by what happens
when the wrong person inherits it

if that answer is terrifying
keep building
