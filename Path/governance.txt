power is borrowed
never owned

legitimacy comes from voluntary participation
no one should be forced to believe
no one should be punished for sincere disagreement
the mission must survive criticism
or it does not deserve to survive

truth comes before certainty
important claims should be tied to evidence
assumptions should be named
predictions should be recorded
when the facts change
the conclusion should be allowed to change

dissent is a feature
not a defect
the strongest disagreement should be heard
before a decision becomes difficult to reverse
critics acting in good faith
are part of the immune system

decisions should be made
as close as possible to the people affected
central authority should remain narrow
only doing what smaller independent groups cannot do well
coordination without unnecessary control

prefer reversible experiments
to irreversible declarations
test ideas at small scale
measure what happens
publish the result
keep what works
discard what does not

never use fear deception or coercion
as a shortcut to agreement
persuasion is not consent
attention is not legitimacy
popularity is not truth

due process must apply
especially when emotions are high
state the accusation
show the evidence
allow a response
separate judgment from retaliation
make appeal possible

the books should be open
the rules should be open
the reasoning should be open
conflicts of interest should be disclosed
power should be auditable

leaders are temporary stewards
authority should expire unless renewed
succession should be ordinary
not a crisis
the system should work
when its founders are gone

judge the project by what it does
not by what it calls itself
improvement should mean
more human freedom
more useful creation
less coercion
less waste
and a wider ability for people
to choose their own lives
